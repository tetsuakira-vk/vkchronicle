LiZ carved out a distinct presence in Tokyo's underground visual kei circuit by building their sound around a core tension between melodic fragility and aggressive instrumental dynamics. Formed in June 2005, the band—featuring vocalist Rei, guitarist Kai (who joined in July 2006), and drummer Ryo—quickly became fixtures at the capital's most influential live venues, from the intimate confines of Takadanobaba AREA to the mid-sized stages of Meguro Rokumeikan and Ikebukuro CYBER.

The band's early output revealed an evolving artistic vision. Their 2005 debut Manatsu NO Yoru NO Yume, later re-released in 2008, established foundational themes that would define their catalogue. Throughout 2006, LiZ demonstrated prolific creativity across multiple releases—Ran, Crying to roar, RED-CHAIN, and depression butterfly each showcased subtle shifts in their approach to arrangement and vocal delivery. By 2007, when they secured a deal with Speed-disk and appeared on the label's compilation volumes, LiZ had matured into a band capable of balancing atmospheric vulnerability with punishing crescendos. That same year saw a remarkable string of releases: Satsuki, IXIon, and L-subliminal- arrived alongside the more viscerally titled Screaming Out Loud, demonstrating the band's refusal to settle into formulas.

The years 2008–2009 represented LiZ's creative peak, with Ruika~Ruika~ and Ruri no Komoriuta followed by the more expansive RAZOR and inner light. These releases suggested a band reaching toward greater sonic sophistication, yet their trajectory was interrupted when the group paused activities in September 2009 after guitarist Yuh and bassist Vict left. LiZ's final performance, "THE LAST IMPULSE" at Ikebukuro BlackHole on September 28, 2010, marked their dissolution, though a one-day revival show at the same venue on December 5, 2012, offered devoted fans a bittersweet reunion.

While LiZ's lifespan was relatively brief in the context of Japanese rock longevity, their influence persisted through their members' subsequent work: Kai joined DOPES., LOVE LOCK, and TRNTY D:CODE, while Ryo contributed to DOPES. and Rs:ARFT. For Western fans discovering the Tokyo visual kei underground of the mid-2000s, LiZ remains essential listening—a band that navigated artistic growth with genuine ambition across their four-year active period, leaving behind a catalogue that continues to reward deep listening.
