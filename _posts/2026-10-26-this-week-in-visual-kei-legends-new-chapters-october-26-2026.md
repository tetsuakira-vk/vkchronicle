What a week for Visual Kei! We've got legacy acts celebrating major milestones, fresh releases firing on all cylinders, and some genuinely unexpected career moves keeping us all on our toes. October's closing strong.

Let's start with the veterans. KISAKI's 50th birthday milestone show is now available on-demand—a chance to witness one of Visual Kei's most influential figures still commanding a room. Meanwhile, ClearVeil just announced they're reuniting for a 20th anniversary one-man live in 2027, which is the kind of news that hits different when you've been following a band that long. And yes, GLAY dropped their 18th album "Tabisuru Riyuu wo Machingara," because GLAY gonna GLAY.

The new release cycle is packed. Verde/ unveiled "Pale Black/" as their latest single, paired with a 3rd anniversary tour that'll keep Alice Nine. fans engaged. CANARY COMPLEX surprise-dropped "BEL ESPRIT" earlier this week—always nice when a band keeps you guessing. w.o.d. is going big with a massive double album announcement alongside their new single "Feelin'," and SUPER★DRAGON landed a drama theme song placement with "Honnou," proving Visual Kei's still got mainstream pull.

Live events and tour announcements are everywhere. Jupiter's announced their European return for 2027 and revealed album concepts in a killer interview. The THIRTEEN wrapped up their year with a Christmas Eve band show and Tokyo year-end blowout. And Hxro's bringing back their beauty seminars at 44—because Visual Kei culture isn't just about the music, it's about the whole aesthetic package.

The wildcard? Golden Bomber's Atsushi Utahiroba just debuted solo with "Become a Horse Owner"—because only in Visual Kei can you have a track with that title and have people take it completely seriously. And FreoMotto launched ANOSORA, a bold new idol supergroup that's got us curious about where this experiment goes.

It's been a week that reminds you why Visual Kei's fanbase stays loyal: you get established legends still pulling weight, mid-tier bands building momentum, fresh projects testing boundaries, and enough surprises to keep the community buzzing. The diversity alone—from classical milestone celebrations to idols to drama tie-ins—shows how much space this scene actually has.

Keep your eyes on Jupiter's European tour rollout and whatever Verde/ has cooking for that anniversary run.
