As DEXCORE gear up to celebrate a decade in the Visual Kei scene this October, the band has revealed plans to cap off 2026 with a pair of extra headline shows in Japan's major metropolitan regions.

For those unfamiliar with the outfit, DEXCORE has carved out a distinctive niche within loud and heavy Visual Kei, evolving their sound across ten years of relentless touring and experimentation. Their uncompromising approach to combining metallic aggression with theatrical presentation has earned them a devoted following both domestically and internationally.

The two new dates—dubbed "Beyond the Storm TOUR【Extra Show】"—come as a natural extension of the band's sprawling tour that ran from June through the summer, which saw them share bills with acts including Azami, Earthists., Graupel, HIKAGE, HOTOKE, Mirrors, View From The Soyuz, NOISEMAKER, and マザリ.

**The headline shows fall on December 21st at Osaka's Umeda ODYSSEY and December 22nd at Nagoya's ell.FITS ALL**, with doors opening at 18:00 and performances commencing at 18:30 both nights. The band will be joined by support members guitarist Ryūya, bassist TAIYO, and drummer MAKI.

But that's not all: DEXCORE will mark their actual tenth anniversary on **October 28th with a special one-man show at Tokyo's Ebisu LIQUIDROOM**, titled "This Is Not a Sad Song." The title itself hints at a reflective but defiant look back across a turbulent decade.

Ticket pricing for the December shows ranges from ¥4,500 (general standing) to ¥9,000 (FC VIP), with U-22 discounts available. Early access kicks off September 17th via the band's official fanclub portal, followed by general pre-orders beginning October 10th.

Between now and January 2027, DEXCORE will also appear at multiple festival slots and collaborative events, including a three-way lineup with DEVILOOF and JILUKA early next year.

Head to DEXCORE's official website and social channels—Instagram, X, and TikTok—for the latest announcements and ticket information.
