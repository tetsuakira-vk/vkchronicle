Only six months into their career, 黒蜜 (Kuromitsu) has already achieved what many Visual Kei bands spend years working toward: a sold-out headline show at one of Tokyo's most prestigious mid-size venues, and the momentum to announce a second nationwide tour before their first one even concludes.

The four-piece—vocalist 乃亜, guitarist 龍一郎, bassist SAM, and drummer 伊織—launched in May 2026 with an ambitious vision: tour all 47 Japanese prefectures within their first year. That strategy is clearly paying off. All 300 tickets for their December 19th final show at Shibuya-REX sold out completely, and the performance will double as a birthday celebration for frontman 乃亜.

But here's where things get really interesting. Rather than taking a breather after wrapping their debut tour, 黒蜥 has already committed to doing the entire 47-prefecture circuit *again* in 2027, under the tour title "2回目の告白" (Second Confession). The new run kicks off in January and stretches through May, hitting everything from intimate regional venues to larger city halls depending on local demand.

The band's ambitions extend beyond the tour grind. They've openly stated their goal is to eventually headline the legendary Nippon Budokan—one of Japanese rock's most coveted stages. Given their trajectory, that aspiration doesn't sound unrealistic.

If you've somehow missed 黒蜜 so far, their music videos for "飼い猫" (Pet Cat) and "君のハーフツインを食べたい" offer a solid introduction to their theatrical, emotionally charged sound. They're the kind of band that understands both spectacle and genuine musicianship—something that clearly resonates with the audiences showing up to their shows.

For tour dates, ticket information, and to follow their journey, head to their official website at kuromitsu.net. You can also find them on YouTube, X, Instagram, and TikTok. If you're in Japan or planning to visit, catching one of these shows while they're still building their fanbase is highly recommended.
