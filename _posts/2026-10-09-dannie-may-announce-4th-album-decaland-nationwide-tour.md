Dannie May just laid down a major announcement at their YOKAI tour finale—and it's a banner moment for the theatrical outfit. The band revealed their fourth full-length album, *DECALAND*, dropping February 10, 2027, along with an ambitious nationwide tour and the launch of their first official fan club.

For those still getting to know them, Dannie May channels bold visual aesthetics with a sound that bridges melodic intensity and experimental edge. They've been building momentum steadily, and this announcement signals a significant step forward in scale and reach.

![Dannie May new logo and artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F09123955%2F263391_Main_DannieMay_logo4-04.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

*DECALAND* will contain ten tracks total: three previously released songs ("Arrogance," "Negative Junkie," and "BAD Gene") plus seven brand-new cuts. The album arrives in three editions—a limited MAYLAND fan club exclusive with special items, an initial press limited version, and a standard edition. The pricier tiers include a Blu-ray featuring the complete live footage from today's Zepp DiverCity final show.

The accompanying tour, *ONEMAN TOUR 2027 "DECALAND,"* spans nine cities nationwide—Hiroshima, Kanazawa, Sendai, Nagoya, Fukuoka, Sapporo, Osaka, Takamatsu, and Tokyo—running from late February through May 6, 2027. The Tokyo finale at Zepp Haneda marks the band's largest-scale national tour to date.

![DECALAND tour key visual](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F09124035%2F263391_Dannie-May-DECALAND-TOUR-KEY-VISUAL-FIX-727x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The newly launched fan club, **MAYLAND**, opens today with immediate ticket presales. Members gain access to exclusive photos, videos, radio content, live streams, and artist talk sessions. Early sign-ups (by November 9, 2026) for annual memberships receive a physical membership card and limited keychain as bonus incentives.

Head to the official *DECALAND* special site (decaland.danniemay-official.com) for full album details and pre-order information coming soon. MAYLAND memberships and ticket presales are live now at mayland-dannie.com—don't sleep on early access if you're planning to catch them on the road.
