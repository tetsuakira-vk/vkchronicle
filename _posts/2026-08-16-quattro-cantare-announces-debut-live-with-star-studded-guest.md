After building momentum through a series of cinematic video releases, the ambitious string-driven project Quattro Cantare is finally taking the stage. Multi-instrumentalist Shigure—a violinist and bassist known for pushing genre boundaries—is bringing his distinctive 4strings project to life with a debut concert titled "Quattro Cantare Live 2027 'Moonlight'" on January 28, 2027, at Tokyo's Akasaka ReNY alpha.

The timing makes the event even more special: Shigure will be celebrating his birthday on stage, marking a milestone moment for the project.

Since launching, Quattro Cantare has captured attention through collaborations with some of the Visual Kei scene's most respected names. The "Ave Maria feat. KAMIJO" video alone has surpassed 700,000 views on YouTube, introducing the project's operatic sensibilities to a broad international audience. Previous releases have featured MORI (wyse), Ju-ken, and HIROKI (D), alongside tracks featuring 苑 (Matentou Opera, Unmei Kousa Ten) and 輝喜 (Antique -Kohii Ten-). Each has built a compelling aesthetic that blends classical arrangements with modern Visual Kei energy.

This inaugural live performance promises to translate that cinematic vision into a full sensory experience. The guest musician roster reads like a who's-who of Japan's underground scene: vocalist IKUO (Rayflower, BULL ZEICHEN 88), vocalist 苑, guitarist MORI (wyse), drummer LEVIN (La'cryma Christi), and pianist おおくぼ けい (Urban Garde). The lineup alone suggests Quattro Cantare has positioned itself at a crossroads where classical musicianship meets avant-garde rock.

Tickets are available through e-plus in two tiers: ¥12,000 (VIP) and ¥7,000 (general admission). Sales began August 16 and are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

For full details, setlist updates, and behind-the-scenes content as the date approaches, head to Quattro Cantare's official X account and YouTube channel. Shigure's personal website is also your best source for project news and artist history.
