Eight-member idol unit Pastel Closet is stepping into a major milestone: their first CD single drops January 26, 2027, barely eighteen months after their formation.

For those new to the group, Pastel Closet emerged from the TIF de Debut 2025 auditions last August under the HEROINES agency—home to the increasingly influential "iLiFE!" project. Operating from a Kanto base, the unit has already built genuine momentum through their versatile live performances, blending emotionally charged material with upbeat idol pop sensibilities. Their group concept, "Each time you open, excitement overflows," sets an optimistic tone that's clearly resonating with growing audiences.

The debut single, "Eternal Startline" (永遠のスタートライン), functions as both group anthem and personal statement. Framed as an encouraging anthem for dreamers pushing through obstacles, the track mirrors Pastel Closet's own journey from audition room to record label. It's the kind of earnest, synced-with-reality approach that idol fans appreciate—especially when the narrative feels earned rather than manufactured.

The release strategy is characteristically ambitious: nine versions across Type-A through Type-I, beginning with a group ensemble cover (Type-A) and individual member variants for each of the eight performers. Each edition bundles the title track with two exclusive B-sides, totaling nineteen songs across all formats. Pricing and full tracklist details arrive later.

Pre-order events kick off November 15th, concentrated around the Kanto region through late January. Early stops include Ikspiari's Celebration Plaza in Chiba (11/15) and multiple Village Vanguard sessions in Shibuya (11/19 and 11/25), with additional dates rolling out through December.

This is a group worth tracking if you appreciate idol work with genuine emotional substance and live energy. Head to their official channels—their X, Instagram, TikTok, and official profile page at HEROINES' site—for full event details, pre-order links, and upcoming announcements. Visual Kei and idol worlds continue overlapping in fascinating ways, and Pastel Closet sits right in that intersection.
