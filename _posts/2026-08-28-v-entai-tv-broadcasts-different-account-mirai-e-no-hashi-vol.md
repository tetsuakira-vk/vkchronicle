Japan's Visual Kei community is getting a fresh broadcast experience. V-Entai, the pioneering Nagoya-based VK television program, is expanding its reach with an exciting new episode highlighting one of the scene's most dynamic live events.

On September 11, 2026, V-Entai will air footage from DIFFERENT:ACCOUNT pre. "Mirai e no Hashi" (Bridge to the Future) vol.4, supported by V-Entai itself. The live took place on August 16, and this broadcast marks a significant moment for how Western and Japanese VK fans can stay connected to the underground scene—especially outside Tokyo's usual spotlight.

V-Entai has been making waves since its launch on StarCat Cable Network, establishing itself as Nagoya's first dedicated Visual Kei television program. Hosted by personality Airō, the show delivers 15-minute episodes packed with live performance footage, artist interviews, and scene coverage that speaks directly to dedicated fans. The program's simultaneous broadcast across both TV and Niconico streaming means accessibility for viewers across different platforms—a smart move for a fanbase that spans traditional media consumers and digital-native listeners.

The "Mirai e no Hashi" series itself has become a cornerstone event for Nagoya's VK community, functioning as a showcase for emerging and established bands. This fourth installment promises to highlight the creative energy that continues to fuel Japan's visual rock underground.

The episode airs Friday, September 11 at 11 PM JST, with a repeat broadcast scheduled for September 18. If you miss the initial airing, the Niconico channel offers on-demand access, ensuring fans won't have to scramble through complicated schedules.

For those wanting to dive deeper into what V-Entai covers and upcoming broadcasts, head to their official site and follow their social channels. Niconico subscribers can access the full program archive and get notified about future episodes. This is exactly the kind of grassroots coverage that keeps the VK scene thriving beyond major label releases.
