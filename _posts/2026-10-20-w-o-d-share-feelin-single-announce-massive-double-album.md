w.o.d. is building serious momentum heading into late 2026. The Japanese rock outfit has just dropped "Feelin'" as a preview single from their forthcoming double album *Fureру* (Touch), and the track already captures exactly why fans are gravitating toward their maximalist, genre-blending sound.

![w.o.d. band photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F06%2F10140121%2F%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%2586%25E3%2582%25A3%25E3%2582%25B9%25E3%2583%2388%25E5%2586%2599%25E7%259C%259F-3.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

"Feelin'" is a celebratory anthem packed with Hammond organ and rhythmic percussion, built around the philosophy "don't think, feel"—a mantra that's clearly resonated with their audience during recent live dates. It's a dancefloor-ready ode to life itself, unmistakably w.o.d. in its maximalist approach to arrangement and emotional delivery.

The single arrives ahead of *Fureру*, their second major-label full-length, due November 18, 2026. This isn't a modest release: it's a proper 20-track double album that consolidates material from two recent EPs (*grunge is dead.* and *YOU ONLY LIVE ONCE. EP*), plus new cuts including the anime theme "NON-FICTION" and the corporate spot "RISE." The tracklist spans everything from introspective character studies ("アンリ," "栞") to propulsive rockers ("TOKYO CALLING," "BURN MY BEAUTIFUL FANTASY").

![Album artwork preview](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F08165457%2F%25E5%25AE%258C%25E7%2594%259F%25E7%2594%25A3CD_%25E3%2582%25B8%25E3%2583%25A3%25E3%2582%25B1%25E5%2586%2599.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Release formats span three options: a standard 2CD initial run (¥4,500), a lavish limited edition with Blu-ray (live footage from recent tours), poster, and photocard in a box (¥12,000), and a 180-gram double LP on 1/3 speed 33rpm vinyl (¥11,000). 

There's also a cryptic Instagram campaign underway—the band posted a mysterious desktop screen with folder icons linking to new accounts. What's coming remains unclear, but w.o.d. clearly has more surprises in store.

![Instagram campaign teaser](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F20193430%2F%25E3%2581%25B5%25E3%2582%258C%25E3%2582%258B_Files_2.png&w=800&output=jpg)

Pre-orders are now live for all formats. Stream "Feelin'" on your preferred platform and keep tabs on their official Instagram for updates on that mysterious new campaign.
