SUPER★DRAGON are gearing up for major visibility this fall as their latest single "Honnou" has been selected as the opening theme for the TOKYO MX drama *Dekiai ~ Actually, the Strongest Hero ~*, set to premiere October 30, 2026.

The outfit's theatrical rock energy and distinctive sound make them a natural fit for television tie-ups, and this placement comes at a particularly significant moment in the band's career. Beyond the drama appearance, member Ikeda Hyouma has landed a supporting role in the series itself, playing Aikawa Itsuki—described as the "final boss of scumbag men"—opposite M!LK's Yoshida Masato, who headlines his acting debut in this comedic romantic drama.

![SUPER★DRAGON promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F23112602%2FSUPER%25E2%2598%2585DRAGON%25E6%2596%25B0%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%2586%25E3%2582%25A3%25E3%2582%25B9%25E3%2583%2588%25E5%2586%2599%25E7%259C%259F.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

"Honnou" will arrive on streaming platforms the same day as the drama's premiere, giving the track immediate exposure to television audiences. A preview showcasing the song's driving rock instrumentation and kinetic energy has already circulated through social media and the TVer streaming platform, hinting at the explosive vitality fans have come to expect from the band.

The single serves as a lead track from SUPER★DRAGON's highly anticipated major second album *STAR*, arriving December 9, 2026. The limited edition includes a Blu-ray featuring footage from their 2026 "Studio" live tour, plus random trading cards. Pre-orders are live now through their official link.

![Album artwork for STAR](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F23112712%2FSUPER%25E2%2598%2585DRAGON2ndAL_%25E5%2588%259D%25E5%259B%259E%25E7%259B%25A4.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Beyond this, SUPER★DRAGON are preparing album release events across four cities with eleven performances, followed by an ambitious nationwide tour in February 2027 titled *SUPER★DRAGON LIVE TOUR 2027 "house"*. For Western fans looking to support the band, check their official channels for streaming links to "Honnou" and pre-order details for *STAR*—both arriving imminently.
