ano just wrapped up an ambitious milestone: their first-ever nationwide hall tour, capping things off with an unforgettable September 4th performance at Tokyo Garden Theater that proved the band has genuinely leveled up.

The "ano Hall Tour 2026 DUAL DINER" kicked off in March across nine cities, and this final addition show was anything but a curtain call. The band brought out the full production arsenal—strings, dancers, and theatrical staging that transformed the venue into an opulent dining hall, complete with an elaborate long table set. ano performed 19 tracks ranging from signature tracks like "Chu, Diversity" and "Happy Lucky Chappy" to recent film and drama tie-ins, all while serving looks in a dramatically embellished pink and red formal mini-dress.

![ano performing at Tokyo Garden Theater](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F04215554%2Fano_artist-photo-1-1024x768.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

What made this show especially significant was the performance debut of a brand-new song written by ano herself. "Love Dive" (ラブにダイブ) first premiered at a fan club exclusive unplugged show in July, but the September 4th performance marked its first full arrangement featuring the band and string section. The track launched on streaming September 5th at midnight, giving fans instant access to the complete version.

The setlist struck a careful balance between brutality and beauty—heavy moments featuring ano's shouts and death vocals contrasted sharply with stripped-down acoustic arrangements and string accompaniment that showcased her range as a vocalist. "I Love You, Just Kidding (unplugged)" and "AIDA" proved particularly effective, with ano's raw emotional delivery filling the room.

![Love Dive single cover](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F04220145%2F2026.09.04_22%25E6%2599%2582%25E8%25A7%25A3%25E7%25A6%2581_%25E3%2583%25A9%25E3%2583%2596%25E3%2581%25AB%25E3%2583%2580%25E3%2582%25A4%25E3%2583%2596_jacket-1024x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

But there's more. ano announced her own festival, "Nyan-Oh Festival" (猫吐祭), launching February 28, 2027 at Toyosu PIT in Tokyo. Details are sparse—the lineup is teased as "ano and more"—but fan club members got first access to presales. She's also launching an official app that will handle ticket pre-sales, exclusive content, and push notifications for announcements.

The full September 4th performance streams exclusively on U-NEXT starting November 14, 2026.

Head to ano's official channels and streaming platforms for "Love Dive" and festival details.
