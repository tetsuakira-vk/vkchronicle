黒夢 (Kuroyume) is bringing high fashion and visual rock aesthetics together with Japanese streetwear brand NEEDLES in an unexpected collaboration that's already live for Western fans willing to order internationally.

The legendary gothic rock act has partnered with NEEDLES to create a limited-edition tracksuit line that fuses the band's distinctive dark aesthetic with the brand's signature sportswear silhouettes. Available exclusively through the official 黒夢 collaboration web store since November 3, 2026, this collection merges two distinct worlds into something genuinely wearable for everyday VK fans.

![黒夢 × NEEDLES tracksuit collaboration](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F04080607%2Fmain-3-1024x669.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The centerpiece is a black tracksuit trimmed with bronze piping—classic NEEDLES DNA—embroidered with the 黒夢 logo. On the jacket, the embroidery sits below the back collar; on the pants, it's placed on the back pocket. The result is understated yet unmistakably band-branded, letting the wearer flex their allegiance without screaming it from the rooftops.

What makes this drop particularly appealing is the pant options. Beyond standard straight and bootcut cuts, NEEDLES' signature voluminous H.D. pants are available, giving fans real silhouette variety depending on their personal style. Whether you prefer a sleek fit or that exaggerated, statement-making volume, there's something here.

![Tracksuit detail shots](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F04080651%2Fsub6-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Pricing sits at 28,000 yen (approximately $185 USD) for the jacket and 25,000 yen ($167 USD) for pants, with mix-and-match options available. Stock is strictly limited, so hesitation could mean missing out.

If you're keen to grab one before they sell out, head directly to the official 黒夢 store at https://kuroyume.online/. The band is also gearing up for their 2027 Zepp Tour—another solid reason to rep their merch while catching them live.
