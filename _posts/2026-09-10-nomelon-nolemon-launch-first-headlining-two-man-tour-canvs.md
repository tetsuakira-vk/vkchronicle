NOMELON NOLEMON is stepping into new territory with their inaugural headlining two-man live series, "CANVS"—and they're bringing some compelling lineup choices along for the ride.

The Tokyo date on October 24th, 2026 will pair them with なとり (Natori) at Zepp Shinjuku, while the Osaka leg on November 3rd sees them joined by fellow visual act the cabs at BIGCAT. For a band mounting their first self-produced touring venture, the strategic pairing with these artists signals confidence and a clear vision for what "CANVS" represents as a creative statement.

![NOMELON NOLEMON promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2025%2F07%2F01161034%2F%25E2%2598%2585NOMELON-NOLEMON_A.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

NOMELON NOLEMON has been quietly building momentum within the visual kei underground, and this move represents a significant milestone in their trajectory. Launching your own tour series is never a small undertaking, and the fact that they've secured two distinct venues with well-regarded co-headliners speaks to their growing pull in the scene.

![the cabs promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F10110742%2Fthe-cabs_A%25E5%2586%2580Main-679x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Both shows run from 17:00 doors / 18:00 showtime, with standing tickets at ¥7,700 plus a separate drink charge. Official presale began September 10th and runs through September 27th via the ticketing link below. Keep in mind the standard terms: no refunds for schedule changes beyond the organizer's control, and absolutely no reselling or transfers allowed.

![CANVS Osaka show poster](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F10110759%2Fno_261103-819x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

On top of "CANVS," NOMELON NOLEMON will also appear at Rock in Japan Festival 2026 on September 19th, and they're part of the NELKE 2MAN TOUR 2026 "MERGE" lineup alongside NELKE on October 27th in Nagoya—so plenty of opportunities to catch them live this season.

Grab your tickets through the official presale at https://l-tike.com/nmnl/ and stay tuned to NOMELON NOLEMON's official channels for any additional tour announcements. This is the kind of grassroots momentum that defines the visual kei scene.
