A living legend continues to rewrite visual kei history. KISAKI—the bassist and mastermind who has shaped the scene for over three decades—has announced a second memorial album and an additional Tokyo performance, extending his milestone 50th birthday celebration into 2026.

KISAKI's influence on visual kei cannot be overstated. As a founding figure and consistent innovator, he's collaborated across countless projects and mentored generations of musicians. His recent "BEYOND THE KINGDOM -Fest of Excellence-" event at Osaka's BIG CAT sold out completely, but overwhelming fan demand for additional performances has led to this encore announcement.

**The New Album & Star-Studded Lineup**

![KISAKI 50th Memorial Album artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph2.kisaki_50th_memorial_flier_ol%25E3%2581%25AE%25E3%2582%25B3%25E3%2583%2594%25E3%2583%25BC-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The second memorial album, *Lost in Elysium*, drops September 30th with the thematic concept: "Broken prayers turn to ash and accumulate in paradise." This 12-track collection features new songs, re-recordings, remixes, and instrumentals. The guest roster spans visual kei and metal worlds alike: Naoki Hashimoto (OUTRAGE), KIBA (Gargoyle), En (Matenrou Opera), Danchō (NoGoD), ryo, and others. Guitarist SAKI from Mary's Blood and Nemophila also contributes.

**Live Events**

The October 24th Tokyo show at BLAZE GOTANDA marks KISAKI PROJECT feat. ryo's likely final appearance, with the full lineup including defspiral, THE BEETHOVEN, NEGA, ClearVeil, and more. A massive closing jam session is promised.

![Tokyo Encore Event](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph3.kisaki_50th_encore_event_flier-02%25E3%2581%25AE%25E3%2582%25B3%25E3%2583%2594%25E3%2583%25BC-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

December 20th wraps the celebration with "Glory and Defeat Born from Miracles Led by Broken Thoughts" at Roppongi GT LIVE TOKYO—featuring a high-volume film concert, talk show with Roppongi Vidoll's Lame and SHOXX editor Kuniaki Suzuki, and photo opportunities.

**Ticket & Release Info**

*Lost in Elysium* costs ¥4,400; pre-orders include the unreleased CD "Insanity." October 24th tickets (¥7,000 advance / ¥7,300 day-of) go on sale June 6th via e-plus. December 20th tickets (¥4,000) open August 22nd.

For full details on purchasing and venue information, head to KISAKI's official site and check e-plus and ticket.net for live bookings. This is essential viewing for anyone invested in visual kei's heritage.
