The THIRTEEN are keeping fans busy this holiday season with a pair of exciting collaborations that blend music, fashion, and exclusive experiences. The Visual Kei outfit—known for their dynamic sound and member creativity beyond the band—has announced festive initiatives from vocalist Mao and guitarist Mizuki that deserve your attention.

**Mao's Luxury Jewelry Debut**

Vocalist Mao is stepping into the fashion realm with a prestigious collaboration. The luxury jewelry brand Lord Camelot has partnered with Mao to release the "Collaboration X'mas Jewelry 2026" collection, dubbed "SNOWFLAKE." The line features two styles of earrings inspired by winter snowflake imagery, combining Lord Camelot's signature refined craftsmanship with elegant, understated design—perfect for the holiday season.

To celebrate the launch, two novelty events will take place in Japan this December. The Tokyo event happens December 24 at Club Phase in Takadanobaba (1 PM–4 PM), while the Osaka event runs December 25 at Hankyu Men's Osaka 5F (1 PM–4 PM, with product exchange until 3:30 PM). Full product details are coming soon.

**Mizuki's Fashion Exhibition & Intimate Live**

![THE MALEVOLENT EDUCATION collection preview](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2FTME26_12-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Meanwhile, Mizuki is hosting a special dual event centered on THE MALEVOLENT EDUCATION, the fashion brand she co-creates with Sadie's guitarist. On December 20 at Tokyo's Sakura Shimmachi NEIGHBOR venue, fans can experience both the 18th Collection exhibition (themed "One's Justice, Another's Evil") and an exclusive Club 13 fan club live.

The exhibition runs 2 PM–4:30 PM, featuring new and archived pieces. Shoppers who spend ¥30,000+ receive a one-on-one meet time with Mizuki plus a 2-shot Cheki photo—and a branded PVC tote bag as a gift.

![THE PLUGLESS M's Limited performance details](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F26_12-580x821.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Following the exhibition, "THE PLUGLESS M's LIMITED vol.18" kicks off at 6:30 PM—an intimate, Club 13 member-only live showcasing Mizuki's music stripped down to vocals and piano. Previous editions have sold out; tickets (¥5,500, plus drink charge) go on sale September 30 at 10 PM via the official shop, with member number verification required at checkout.

For more details on both events, check The THIRTEEN's official channels and Club 13's ticketing page at shop13.stores.jp.
