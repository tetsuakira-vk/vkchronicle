kurayamisaka has just secured a massive milestone: their biggest-capacity solo performance will now reach fans across the entire country. The Tokyo-based visual kei act announced a nationwide live viewing event for their October 17, 2026 show at Zepp DiverCity, bringing this landmark concert to 31 cinema locations and one live venue.

For those unfamiliar with kurayamisaka, the band has built a devoted following through their theatrical dark aesthetic and the playful ambiguity embedded in their ongoing "tte, doko?" concert series—a name that translates roughly to "what is this?" They've cultivated a signature sound that blends visual kei's elaborate presentation with introspective songwriting, making them essential listening for fans drawn to the movement's more experimental corners.

![kurayamisaka promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F05111856%2Fkurayamisaka_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E5%2586%2589_%25E3%2583%2588%25E3%2583%25AA%25E3%2583%259F%25E3%2583%25B3%25E3%2582%25B0%25E5%258F%25AF.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The Zepp DiverCity performance represents a serious step forward for the band—both the main October 17 show and an additional October 24 date at Gorilla Hall Osaka sold out immediately upon ticket release, which speaks volumes about their growing momentum. Now, fans unable to secure in-person tickets can experience the livestream in theaters and venues nationwide.

The live viewing event—officially titled "Theatrical Version kurayamisaka tte, doko? #8 'Zepp DiverCity Solo Performance Edition' (Live Broadcast)"—kicks off at 6:00 PM JST. Ticket pricing is set at ¥3,300 (approximately $22 USD). An early-bird lottery presale through eplus begins today and runs through September 13.

![Live viewing promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F31144244%2FkurayamisakaLVKV-1024x576.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Venues span from Sapporo to Matsuyama, ensuring accessibility across Japan. This marks kurayamisaka's first official live viewing release—a fitting recognition of their expanded fanbase and the band's evolution into one of visual kei's more compelling contemporary acts.

If you're keen to catch this historic performance, head to the eplus presale link immediately. For more information on kurayamisaka's releases and upcoming shows, check their official channels and streaming platforms.
