When MUCC decided to move their curated festival deeper into heartland Japan, they weren't just changing venues—they were bringing the entire Visual Kei family home. The opening night of *LuV Together 2026* at Mito Civic Hall in Ibaraki proved exactly why this event has become essential pilgrimage territory for serious VK fans: it's where the movement's elder statesmen pass the torch to the next generation, all bound by something more powerful than mere nostalgia.

Now in its third iteration, *LuV Together* has evolved into a two-day showcase that celebrates what MUCC themselves have always embodied—the idea that Visual Kei is fundamentally about *love*. Love for the aesthetic, for the craft, and for the bonds that connect bands across generations. Day one delivered on that promise spectacularly.

![XANVALA bringing theatrical energy to the Mito stage](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F11181827%2F1_XANVALA_1-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Rising stars XANVALA opened the evening with their maiden Mito performance, their red-and-white coordinated aesthetic and soaring melodies proving that the new generation isn't just inheriting Visual Kei—they're actively reviving it with purpose. Vocalist Takumi's command was remarkable for a debut in such company, delivering both delicate moments and aggressive breakdowns with genuine presence.

What followed was a masterclass in VK lineage. Merry—MUCC's literal contemporaries from the same scene—arrived to celebrate their 25th anniversary with a setlist that honored both history and current momentum. Their secret surprise? A breathtaking cover of MUCC's "Aka," complete with Gara's theatrical interpretation. The moment wasn't just a tribute; it was a reminder of how these bands have remained tethered through three decades of Japanese rock's evolution.

![D'ESPAIRSRAY commanding the darkness](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F11181748%2F6_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25B3%25E3%2582%25B3%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%25AB05-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

D'ESPAIRSRAY's return—their first *LuV Together* appearance since their 2024 reformation—was nothing short of cathartic. Having conquered international stages during their original run alongside MUCC, their reunion feels less like a nostalgic callback and more like a necessary recalibration of what Japanese Visual Kei means on the global stage. HIZUMI's evolved vocal approach and Karyu's signature guitar work felt sharper, more refined—proof that resurrection doesn't mean regression.

The energy DEZERT carried into their set seemed to bridge one final gap before MUCC's headlining turn, validating their second consecutive year at the festival and setting the table for what promises to be an unforgettable second night.
