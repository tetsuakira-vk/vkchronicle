Visual Kei royalty KISAKI just gave fans worldwide a second chance to experience his landmark 50th birthday celebration concert—and it's coming straight to your screen.

The legendary guitarist and founding member of Phantasmagoria orchestrated an unforgettable evening at Tokyo's BLAZE GOTANDA on October 24th, 2026, bringing together a stacked lineup of contemporary and classic VK talent under the banner "BEYOND THE KINGDOM -Final Masquerade-." Demand for footage was immediate and overwhelming, so organizers have now locked in an on-demand streaming window to capture every moment of the sold-out event.

KISAKI's influence on Visual Kei's evolution cannot be overstated. As a pioneering force across multiple decades, he's shaped the sound and aesthetic of the scene through both his own projects and collaborations with emerging artists. This special showcase perfectly encapsulated his legacy—featuring performances from NETH PRIERE CAIN, Phobia, 椎名ひかり (Hikari Shiina), ClearVeil, THE BEETHOVEN, NEGA, defspiral, and a KISAKI PROJECT set fronted by vocalist ryo and featuring guitarist HIZAKI. The event culminated in a massive collaborative session performing "神歌" (Kamigoe).

The full concert captures every band's complete setlist plus that landmark jam session—a comprehensive package for both those who made the journey to Tokyo and those watching from afar.

**Here's what you need to know:**

Tickets are priced at ¥5,000 (approximately $35 USD). The standard viewing window runs from October 25–31, with an optional "+Archive" extension available through November 24th. You can purchase tickets through October 24th–November 24th via the official ticketing platform.

This is a genuine opportunity to witness a living legend's milestone moment with some of Visual Kei's brightest contemporary voices. Head to the official ticketing page to secure your access before the sale closes—these milestone concert streams rarely get second chances.
