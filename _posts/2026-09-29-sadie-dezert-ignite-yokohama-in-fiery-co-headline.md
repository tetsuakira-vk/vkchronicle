When Sadie emerged from hiatus in 2024, the visual kei legends didn't just slip quietly back into the scene—they've been building something purposeful. On September 22nd at Yokohama Bay Hall, the band hosted the second installment of their annual "THE UNITED KILLERS" event, bringing fellow heavyweights DEZERT along for a two-band assault that proved why these acts remain essential voices in contemporary Japanese metal.

The evening began with DEZERT, fresh off releasing new tracks "Welcome To The End" and "TOXIC." Vocalist Senshuu immediately commanded the room with a promise: "We're here to burn our souls for you." The band tore through their setlist with surgical precision, balancing gut-punch heaviness with melodic hooks that cut deep. New material sat confidently alongside staples like "君の子宮を触る" (remixed with heartfelt lyrical tweaks), while standouts like "セイオン" and "MONSTER" showcased the kind of light-and-dark duality that makes DEZERT's 15-year legacy undeniable. The kinetic energy escalated through "感染少女" and "秘密," with Senshuu completely surrendering himself to the performance—crowdsurfing, drenched, unguarded. Before handing the stage over, he made a pilgrimage confession: Sadie's live set from his university days was the moment that changed everything for him.

![Sadie performing at THE UNITED KILLERS 2026](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F29195335%2F20260922_THEUNITEDKILLER_%25E6%25A8%25AA%25E6%25B5%259CBAYHALL%2540jvnphoto168-20262026092210933-683x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Sadie followed with the ceremonial gravitas they've perfected—a wall of sound anchored by Shao's drums and punctuated by Makoto's unmistakable vocal assault. The classics "迷彩" and "陽炎" hit like body blows, but the real triumph was the live debut of "悪の花," their first new song in eleven years. Written by guitarist Tsurugi, the track gallops with infectious energy while preserving every ounce of Sadie's existential weight. It's already surpassed 500,000 YouTube views—a testament to how hungry fans were for new material.

The night's wildest moment came during "Grieving the dead soul" when Senshuu literally stormed the stage to deliver guest vocals. What could've felt gimmicky instead became genuinely moving—watching a vocalist and his hero perform shoulder-to-shoulder, calling back and forth to the crowd in real-time communion. Sadie closed with a two-song climax that left everyone breathless, culminating in an extraordinary "迷彩" finale where Senshuu was literally elevated to solo stardom, performing the track as if it were his own meditation on survival.

Sadie announced a massive 18-venue, 20-show tour titled "THE BLEEDING BLACK MASS," kicking off January 8th in bassist Mizuki's hometown of Shiga. They're carrying a new album, "PANDRA," due in December, plus a 22nd-anniversary concert at Tokyo's Zepp Shinjuku on March 21st.
