[Alexandros] are celebrating 15 years in the music industry with a landmark pop-up store experience that captures the band's entire journey in one physical space. Opening this September in Tokyo's Harajuku district, the activation doubles as a celebration of their milestone anniversary live performance—and yes, the cheeky subtitle acknowledges they're already creeping into year 16.

For those unfamiliar with the band, [Alexandros] emerged onto the Japanese rock scene in 2011, blending energetic alt-rock with infectious melodies and a refreshingly unpretentious approach to stagecraft. Over the past decade and a half, they've built a dedicated fanbase both domestically and internationally, becoming one of Japan's most consistent and beloved modern rock exports.

![15th Anniversary VIP PARTY '25 live performance](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F05%2F29140544%2F25.12_A_Photo_M.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The pop-up store runs September 3–8, 2026 at Universal Music Store Harajuku (1F and 3F). The first floor showcases an archival timeline spanning from their Universal Music debut with "Watari Dori / Dracula La" through their current output, supplemented by lyric-based installations that immerse visitors in the band's aesthetic and messaging.

![Pop-up store promotional visual](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F13165007%2FAlexandros-POPUP_KV_A3_RGB-724x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The third floor is where the merch lives—exclusive items created specifically for this event, including t-shirts featuring iconic Universal Music CD jacket artwork, stickers, miniature CD keychains, and enamel pins. Interactive elements include a custom keychain station, a receipt-style photo booth, and a participatory "ultimate setlist" feature where fans can rearrange [Alexandros]' biggest tracks into their personal dream concert order.

![Available merchandise lineup](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F13165028%2F%25E3%2582%25B0%25E3%2583%2583%25E3%2582%25BA%25E4%25B8%2580%25E8%25A6%25A7_Alexandros_260812_OL-01-4-725x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

**Visitor Information**

Location: Universal Music Store Harajuku, 1-20-6 Jingumae, Shibuya Ward, Tokyo (3 minutes walk from Harajuku Station, Takeshita Exit)  
Hours: 11:00 AM–8:00 PM daily  
Website: https://store-harajuku.universal-music.co.jp/

Advance reservations are required September 3–6 (all day) and September 7–8 (11:00 AM–2:00 PM). Free walk-in slots will be available September 7–8 from 2:00 PM onward, subject to capacity. Register for tickets via LivePocket (free membership required): https://livepocket.jp/

The accompanying video release, "[Alexandros] 15th Anniversary VIP PARTY '25," documents the band's December 2025 two-night celebration concert at Toyota Arena Tokyo, featuring performances with former drummers—a genuinely special moment captured for posterity.

For the latest on [Alexandros], head to their official channels and social media for tour announcements and upcoming releases.
