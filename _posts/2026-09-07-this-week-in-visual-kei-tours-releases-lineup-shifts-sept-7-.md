It's been a massive week in Visual Kei, folks—the kind where you can barely keep up with all the announcements flying in. We're talking tour expansions, major lineup news, fresh releases, and enough momentum to carry us straight into autumn. Buckle up.

Let's start with the touring circuit, because it's absolutely packed right now. Kuroyume wrapped their arena run with a triumphant Tokyo finale that fans are still raving about, bringing their massive tour full circle. On the flip side, ano just closed out their own hall tour and are celebrating with a new single, "Love Dive"—a solid statement of intent as they look ahead. VINYL made headlines by heading to Hokkaido for their first regional tour, proving the acoustic wave is spreading beyond the capital. And looking further down the road, Maki just announced a full nationwide 2027 tour *plus* snagged a slot at KUZIRA Festival, so circle that on your calendar immediately.

The release side of things is equally stacked. Kuromitsu dropped fresh photos and the "飼い猫" MV—their visual game is seriously on point. Hump Back revealed full details for their upcoming "Soft Cave" mini album and paired it with a tour announcement that should give you plenty of live opportunities. Ta-rot took a different approach, reviving two classic tracks, 'PSYCHO' and 'Tsurushi-nin,' with a brand new digital release, plus they've got live dates attached. For the solo circuit, Kousuke's going all-in on Halloween energy with an immersive solo show landing October 23—if you're into experimental solo work, mark that one down.

Speaking of lineup shifts: Arlequin's five-piece era officially begins with the arrival of new drummer Ryo, and from all accounts, the chemistry is already firing. That's the kind of refresh that can genuinely reshape a band's sound, so we're curious to see where they take it.

There's a real sense this week that Visual Kei is moving into its next phase—bigger venues, national reach, experimental solo ventures, and bands actively evolving their lineups. It's the sweet spot between established acts owning their lane and newer energy pushing things forward. Next week, we'll be keeping an eye on how these tour announcements start selling out and what those classic Ta-rot tracks reveal about their creative direction moving forward.
