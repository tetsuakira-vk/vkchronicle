Legendary guitarist SUGIZO is stepping into uncharted territory with his first anime soundtrack, and the results promise to be nothing short of spectacular. The *Dark Machine the Animation* original soundtrack arrives December 9, 2026, bringing SUGIZO's signature dark, heavy sonic palette to a new e-sports themed anime series.

For those unfamiliar, SUGIZO is one of Japan's most revered musicians—a founding member of LUNA SEA and X JAPAN guitarist who continues to lead his own projects including the jazz-rock band SHAG. His involvement in anime music marks a significant milestone, and he's brought together an all-star roster of collaborators to realize the project's ambitious soundscape.

![Dark Machine The Animation Official Artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fa-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The soundtrack boasts contributions from an eclectic lineup: MaZDA (longtime COSMIC DANCE QUINTET member), producer Ubar-Tumar, vocaloid producer ATLOS, JILUKA guitarist Sena, Shinobu (Creature Creature/La'cryma Christi), and the full SHAG lineup. This cross-genre assembly reflects SUGIZO's philosophy of creative collaboration and pushes the soundtrack beyond typical anime fare.

The release includes heavy hitters on its theme songs. BUCK∞TICK delivers the opening theme "存在理由 改" ("Reason for Existence -Revised-"), while three代目 J SOUL BROTHERS handle the ending with "Galaxy Mode." An in-universe idol group called STELLATUS contributes the energetic insert track "Pitch Black," which drops early on November 11 ahead of the full OST release.

![Dark Machine The Animation Soundtrack Cover](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fr-580x326.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The physical release comes in multiple formats: a 2CD set with all 40 tracks, 2LP vinyl with SUGIZO's personal selections, and a limited Victor Online Store exclusive bundle featuring a custom record bag. Jacket artwork comes courtesy of mecha designer Hideki Tenjin, known for his work on the *Macros* and *Gundam* series.

To celebrate the launch, SUGIZO and anime director Kazuo Terada will hold a talk and signing event at Tower Records Shibuya on December 9. It's a rare chance to connect with the creative minds behind this ambitious cross-media project.

Head to the official Victor Entertainment store for pre-orders and streaming availability across Apple Music, LINE MUSIC, iTunes, and major download services—all going live December 9 at midnight.
