When Studio Pierrot ended its long silence by greenlight a new magical girl anime, nobody expected a full-circle moment this profound. But that's exactly what's happening this September: Creamy Mami, the original magical girl icon from 1983, is revisiting decades of her studio's genre-defining catalog on a brand new cover album.

"Connecting Mami: Pierrot Magical Girl Cover Songs" isn't just a nostalgia play. It's a genuine collision between eras. Creamy Mami (voiced by Ota Takako, reprising her legendary role) will tackle the opening and insert themes from across the Pierrot magical girl lineage—including past classics like "Magical Fairy Persia," "Magical Star Magical Emi," "Magical Idol Pastel Yumi," and "Magical Stage Fancy Lala." But the real story is what happens in tracks one and seven: full collaborations with the new leads from Studio Pierrot's 2024 comeback series, "The Magical Sisters Luluttolily."

For Western fans unfamiliar with this slice of anime history: Pierrot spent the 1980s essentially inventing the magical girl formula that would define the genre for decades. Creamy Mami herself was the prototype—a sentient magical girl show before Sailor Moon, a template that countless series would follow. To see her voice actress stepping into these songs 40+ years later, alongside the new generation's leads, carries real weight.

The album contains seven tracks total, with two collaboration pieces bookending a solo setlist that spans the studio's entire legacy. Early pressings include an exclusive clear stand figure illustrated by Studio Pierrot themselves.
