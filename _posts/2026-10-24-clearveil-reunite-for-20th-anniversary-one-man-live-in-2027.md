ClearVeil's journey has always been defined by unexpected reunions. The disbanded Tokyo Visual Kei act—originally active in the 2000s—announced their latest chapter during KISAKI's 50th birthday celebration concert on October 24th at BLAZE GOTANDA: a full-band one-man live scheduled for March 13, 2027, at Nagoya's Electric Lady Land (E.L.L.).

The venue choice carries profound symbolism. E.L.L. is where ClearVeil held their original disbandment live in 2010, making this comeback a full-circle moment. The timing couldn't be more poignant: 2027 marks both ClearVeil's 20th anniversary *and* Electric Lady Land's 50th, creating a rare convergence of milestone anniversaries.

Despite their disbanded status, ClearVeil has sporadically reunited over the past decade, though never quite on their own terms until now. All five original members—vocalist Toki, guitarists Nozomi and Ryuto, bassist HISAYOSHI, and drummer Naruka—have confirmed they'll take the stage. According to HISAYOSHI's statement, the band had long harbored desire to produce a proper one-man live under their own volition, independent of festival appearances or tribute events. That vision finally crystallizes in 2027.

The members' comments reveal a band approaching this performance with profound respect for their legacy while refusing to simply recreate the past. Toki emphasized this isn't a nostalgic exercise—it's a platform to "overwrite" their earlier selves with everything they've learned across two decades. The sentiment echoes throughout: they've maintained their musical foundation individually and collectively, and they're ready to prove it.

Tickets range from ¥5,000 (advance) to ¥6,000 (day-of), with limited VIP packages at ¥10,000 offering rehearsal access and group photos. VIP pre-sales begin October 24th at 21:00 JST via e-plus, with general sales opening November 7th at noon.

For Western fans planning a Japan trip next spring or longtime followers of the Visual Kei scene, this represents something increasingly rare: a genuinely meaningful reunion by a band that refuses easy sentimentality.

Head to ClearVeil's official X and Instagram accounts for the latest updates, and secure tickets through e-plus ahead of what promises to be a definitive statement from one of Visual Kei's most thoughtful acts.
