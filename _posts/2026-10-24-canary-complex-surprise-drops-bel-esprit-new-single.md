A surprise arrival from the US-based visual kei realm: CANARY COMPLEX has just unveiled "BEL ESPRIT," a new digital single that extends the sonic and visual narrative of their fictional world, The Imaginary Paris.

If you haven't encountered CANARY COMPLEX yet, here's the essentials: the project centers on Canary Kasey, an American musician deeply rooted in '90s and 2000s visual kei aesthetics who has been crafting something genuinely unique—a fusion of baroque orchestration, French chanson, gothic atmosphere, new wave sensibility, and impressionist color theory. Rather than simply pastiche, these elements interweave into a cohesive artistic vision told through music, painting, costume design, and interconnected storytelling. Think Belle Époque Paris filtered through a visual kei sensibility, with each track adding another chapter to an imaginary realm.

"BEL ESPRIT" arrives as a surprise release on October 23rd, 2026, accompanied by an original music video depicting the song's emotional and conceptual landscape on the same day.

![CANARY COMPLEX visual imagery](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fg-1-580x870.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

What makes this week particularly significant is the convergence of two major moments for Canary Kasey. Just eight days after "BEL ESPRIT" drops, they'll take the stage as guitarist for KAMIJO's New York performance on October 31st—a fitting collision of personal artistic expression and deep respect for the visual kei pioneers who shaped their own creative path.

The track marks another thread in The Imaginary Paris narrative, building on CANARY COMPLEX's already impressive reach across both Japanese and international visual kei communities. The project has gained recognition from outlets like VISUAVI Japan and club Zy., and Canary Kasey is featured in the "World Visual Kei Guidebook" by Tetsuya Mizushina, complete with comments from KAMIJO himself.

For those wanting to experience "BEL ESPRIT" for yourself, head directly to CANARY COMPLEX's official channels: their website at canarycomplex.com hosts links to all streaming platforms and music services. You can also follow their creative journey on Instagram (@canary.complex and @canary.kasey), TikTok (@canary.kasey), YouTube, and X (@CanaryKasey) for behind-the-scenes glimpses into this evolving visual kei universe.
