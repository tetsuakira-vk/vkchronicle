Visual Kei outfit VIVARUSH has secured a major anime placement—their track "Emperor Time" will serve as the opening theme for *Tasuketsu*, marking a significant milestone for the theatrical rock act.

Vocalist Ruimaru recently opened up about the opportunity in an exclusive interview, diving into what drew the band to the project and how the song fits within their artistic vision. For those unfamiliar with VIVARUSH, the group blends the ornate aesthetics and dynamic energy that define visual kei with hard-hitting rock compositions, creating a sound that's equally suited to the concert hall and the screen.

Anime openings have become an important avenue for Japanese rock and metal bands to reach international audiences, and VIVARUSH's placement puts them in good company. The visibility that comes with a slot on a major anime series can introduce Western fans to acts they might otherwise overlook—and given the theatrical nature of visual kei, the pairing feels natural.

In the interview, Ruimaru discusses not only the creation of "Emperor Time" and its thematic connection to *Tasuketsu*, but also reflects on the band's broader ambitions moving forward. For a visual kei group, sustaining creative momentum while building a fanbase outside Japan requires both artistic integrity and strategic opportunities like this one. The anime tie-in suggests VIVARUSH is thinking strategically about visibility without compromising their identity.

Visual kei fans with even a passing interest in anime crossovers should check out "Emperor Time"—it's a solid entry point into what the band has to offer. The full interview provides much deeper insight into Ruimaru's songwriting process and the group's future direction.
