One of Japan's most legendary live venues is preparing for a milestone moment. Shinjuku Loft, the storied club that has shaped the trajectory of Japanese rock for five decades, officially marks its 50th anniversary on October 1st, 2026—counting from its original opening in the Nishi-Shinjuku era. To celebrate, the venue is rolling out an ambitious special event series called *ROCK OF AGES 2026*, running from September 27th through October 6th.

This is more than a nostalgic retrospective. The lineup reflects Shinjuku Loft's enduring relevance across generations of Japanese music: prog-rock institution **Kinnikudan** (筋肉少女帯) opens the celebrations on September 27th with support from Dr. Haseegawa Koji and keyboardist Mie Shibasiro. Days later, acoustic performances from established artists like Yuji Nakada and Sho Wada bridge the gap between yesterday's underground pioneers and today's active scene.

The anniversary truly crescendos on October 1st itself, with a talk-and-live special featuring Keiichi Suzuki and Shunichi Sogabe—two figures whose careers are inseparable from Shinjuku's history.

What sets this celebration apart is its reach beyond a single venue. On October 3rd, Shinjuku Loft orchestrates a massive circuit event spanning nine connected spaces: Zepp Shinjuku, Bar the Loft, Loft/Plus One, Rock Cafe Loft, and several neighboring live houses. This multi-venue format lets fans experience Shinjuku's ecosystem as a unified cultural hub.

The festival closes with **The Cro-Magnons** and Ruffin Nose on October 6th, bringing the week full circle with the punk and rock energy that originally defined the venue's rebellious spirit.

Advance tickets for several shows have already sold out, a testament to how deeply Shinjuku Loft remains embedded in Japan's music consciousness. For complete lineups, venue details, and remaining ticket availability, visit the official 50th Anniversary special site linked from Loft Project's main schedule page.
