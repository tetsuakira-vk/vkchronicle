<h2>August 2026 in Review: A Month of Legends, New Blood, and Surprise Comebacks</h2>

<p>August delivered one of the year's most eclectic months for Visual Kei—a perfect storm of veteran acts reclaiming the spotlight, promising newcomers making bold debuts, and the scene's deep archive finally getting its due.</p>
<!--more-->

<h3>Major Tours & Live Announcements</h3>

<p><strong>The comeback narrative dominated August.</strong> <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/zeke-deux-pack-september-november-with-birthday-tour-europe-/">Zeke Deux packed September through November with a birthday tour, European dates, and Halloween shows</a>, signalling the band's ambitions on the international stage. Meanwhile, <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/z-clear-welcome-new-drummer-y-launch-tour/">Z CLEAR welcomed new drummer Yū and launched their "序曲" tour</a>, breathing fresh energy into an established act. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/jubaku-hyourei-returns-as-full-band-after-hiatus/">Jubaku Hyourei returned as a full band after a hiatus</a>, while <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/kusu-kusu-resurrect-after-30-years-with-new-single/">KUSU KUSU resurrected after 30+ years with a new single</a>—a genuinely jaw-dropping moment for 1980s VK purists.</p>

<p>On the festival front, <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/fest-inazuma-2026-reveals-final-lineup-vk-acts-confirmed/">FEST. INAZUMA 2026 revealed its final lineup with Visual Kei acts confirmed</a>, and <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/hero-babykingdom-host-rengeki-festival-dec-16/">HERO & BabyKingdom announced the "Rengeki" festival for December 16</a>. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/kurayamisaka-zepp-divercity-live-to-stream-nationwide/">kurayamisaka's Zepp DiverCity live will stream nationwide</a>, democratizing access to one of the summer's most anticipated shows.</p>

<h3>New Releases & Creative Directions</h3>

<p><a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/zilqy-drop-fastest-track-yet-from-debut-album-birth-of-riot/">Zilqy dropped the fastest track yet from their debut album "Birth of Riot,"</a> proving heavy Visual Kei remains a vital force. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/04-limited-sazabys-return-with-view-ep-and-tattoo-artist-col/">04 Limited Sazabys returned with the 'VIEW' EP and a tattoo artist collaboration</a>, blending sonic experimentation with visual storytelling. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/go-go-vanillas-release-trickster-for-tokyo-revengers-anime/">go!go!vanillas released "Trickster" for the Tokyo Revengers anime</a>, continuing the tradition of Visual Kei's crossover into mainstream media. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/sadie-return-with-flower-of-evil-announce-september-release/">Sadie returned with "Flower of Evil," announcing a September release</a>, while <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/08/wurts-release-debut-mini-album-spark-with-exclusive-zine/">WurtS released their debut mini album 'SPARK' with an exclusive zine</a>—a refreshing commitment to physical, tactile artistry.</p>
