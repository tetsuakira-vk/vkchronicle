<h2>VK Chronicle Month in Review: September 2026</h2>

<p>September proved that Visual Kei's appetite for anniversaries, international expansion, and bold creative reinvention shows no signs of slowing down—and the scene ate well.</p>
<!--more-->

<h3>Tours & Live Events</h3>

<p>The month was dominated by major touring announcements and electrifying live moments. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/sadie-dezert-ignite-yokohama-in-fiery-co-headline/">Sadie and DEZERT ignited Yokohama in a fiery co-headline</a> that left fans breathless, while <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/kuroyume-completes-arena-circuit-with-perfect-tokyo-finale/">Kuroyume completed their arena circuit with a "perfect" Tokyo finale</a>. International reach expanded considerably: <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/blaze-gears-up-for-first-european-tour-this-october/">BLAZE geared up for their first European tour</a>, while <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/f5ve-add-brisbane-date-to-australia-tour-2026/">f5ve added Brisbane to their Australia circuit</a>. Perhaps most poignantly, <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/inoran-returns-to-asia-after-10-years-grand-final-looms/">INORAN returned to Asia after a decade away</a>, with a grand final on the horizon.</p>

<h3>Milestone Anniversaries & Releases</h3>

<p>September was <em>the</em> month for celebrating legacies. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/mucc-mark-30th-anniversary-with-50th-single-love-letter/">MUCC marked their 30th anniversary with their 50th single, "Love Letter,"</a> a staggering milestone few bands achieve. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/announces-elaborate-20th-anniversary-campaign-with-dual-albu/">浅葱 announced an elaborate 20th anniversary campaign featuring dual albums and a nationwide tour</a>, while <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/shazna-s-melty-love-returns-for-30th-anniversary/">SHAZNA's iconic "Melty Love" returned for their 30th anniversary</a>. <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/l-arc-en-ciel-reissue-12-classic-albums-on-vinyl/">L'Arc-en-Ciel reissued 12 classic albums on vinyl</a>, cementing their status as untouchable legends, and <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/glay-return-with-18th-album-tabisuru-riyuu-wo-machininagara/">GLAY returned with their 18th album, "Tabisuru Riyuu wo Machininagara."</a> Looking ahead, <a href="https://vkchronicle.com/2026/09/9goats-black-out-announce-20th-anniversary-revival-live-in-m/">9GOATS BLACK OUT announced a 20th anniversary revival live for May 2027</a>.</p>
