Ahead of their Halloween-themed one-man tour, visual kei outfit Zera has released exclusive limited-edition visuals that capture the band's theatrical spirit perfectly. The three-piece—featuring vocalist Hisui, guitarist KIRAN, and bassist Ryu—are gearing up for what promises to be a memorable autumn campaign that blends horror aesthetics with the band's signature dark visual identity.

![Zera vocalist Hisui in Halloween costume](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fphhalloween-hisui-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those unfamiliar with Zera, the band exemplifies contemporary Japanese visual kei—delivering moody, guitar-driven compositions wrapped in striking visual presentation. They've been steadily building momentum in the underground scene, and this autumn slate represents a significant expansion of their touring footprint.

The Halloween event, titled "ZERAWEEN NIGHT," hits three major cities across late October. Performances are scheduled for October 27 at Aoyama RizM (Tokyo), October 30 at Imaiike 3STAR (Nagoya), and October 31 at Shinsaibashi soma (Osaka). All shows open at 5 p.m., with performances starting at 5:30 p.m. Advance tickets are ¥4,500; door price is ¥5,000 (drinks separate). The band will perform in special Halloween-exclusive costumes—a visual treat for attendees.

![Zera guitarist KIRAN in Halloween costume](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fphhalloween-kiran-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Beyond the Halloween run, Zera's "Kinetic Horror Show" one-man tour extends well into 2027, with confirmed dates spanning Fukuoka, Urawa, Yokohama, Shizuoka, Osaka, and Shinjuku. Additional festival appearances round out their schedule through January.

![Zera bassist Ryu in Halloween costume](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fphhalloween-ryu-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Tickets for the Halloween shows and subsequent tour dates are available through e+. For the latest on Zera's activities, check their official website at planet-child.jp/zera, follow them on X (@ze_raofficial), or connect via Instagram (@zera_official2020).
