Seek from legendary Visual Kei outfit Psycho le Cému is continuing his long-running Nico Nico Live radio show with a special guest appearance from HIZUMI, who currently performs with both D'espairsRay and NUL.

The 39th episode of "seekのFish Dance Hour" (seek's Fish Dance Hour) airs on **December 2nd at 7 PM JST**. The show, billed as "a discussion of Visual Kei," pairs Seek with regular co-host Seiichi Hoshiko and welcomes HIZUMI as this week's featured guest.

For those unfamiliar with the program, "Fish Dance Hour" has become a staple in the Visual Kei community since its launch, bringing together musicians, producers, and industry figures to reflect on the genre's past, present, and cultural significance. Previous guests have read like a who's-who of the scene: members of Alice Nine., La'cryma Christi, THE MICRO HEAD 4N'S, Royz, Versailles, and many others. The diversity of perspectives—spanning multiple generations and subgenres—keeps the conversation fresh and genuinely engaging.

HIZUMI's appearance is particularly noteworthy. With his work spanning the symphonic darkness of D'espairsRay's classic era through to the avant-garde experimentalism of NUL., he brings a fascinating artistic trajectory to discuss. Fans curious about his creative journey and thoughts on Visual Kei's evolution should definitely tune in.

The broadcast begins as a free stream, but transitions partway through to club Zy channel member-only content (a subscription service at roughly ¥330/month, with the first month free). The full program can be accessed via the club Zy channel, which also offers archived nico broadcasts, exclusive artist interviews, behind-the-scenes photography, and priority ticket access to the "stylish wave" event series.

**Tune in on December 2nd at 7 PM JST via the official broadcast link.** Whether you're a longtime Psycho le Cému fan or simply interested in unfiltered conversations about Visual Kei culture, this episode offers genuine insight beyond the usual band press cycle.
