Aooo are cementing their rise through Japan's live circuit with their first-ever video release, capturing a stellar performance from their inaugural Zepp tour. The band will drop *Aooo Live Tour "RINGRING" at Zepp Haneda (TOKYO)* on December 2, 2026—a Blu-ray and CD package documenting their May 29 show at Tokyo's Zepp Haneda venue.

![Aooo artist photo 2026](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F03%2F18185718%2F%25E2%2598%2585Aooo_artistphoto_2026.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those still getting acquainted with Aooo, the four-piece have carved out a distinctive niche in the contemporary visual rock landscape with their high-energy aesthetic and catchy, hook-laden songwriting. Their theatrical approach and vibrant stage presence have quickly earned them a devoted fanbase—and this release proves why live shows have become central to their identity.

The package runs the full setlist across both formats: all 20 tracks performed that night, including standouts like "CALL," "Geeek," "CRAZZZY," and "ポラリス" (Polaris). The Blu-ray includes uncut live footage, while the CD captures the complete audio with crystal clarity, letting fans relive both the visual spectacle and sonic punch of the evening.

![Aooo Live Tour RINGRING package artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F30163305%2FAoooRINGRING_PKG-771x1024.png&w=800&output=jpg)

Pricing sits at ¥8,800 (tax included). Multiple retailers are offering limited-run bonuses—Amazon has a 3-piece visual sheet set, HMV is bundling bromides, and Tower Records, Rakuten Books, and other chains each have exclusive merch tied to pre-orders. Stock on these is limited, so early birds will have their pick.

In parallel, Aooo have announced a headlining show at Tokyo's legendary Nippon Budokan on November 22, 2026—no small feat for any band. The final lottery pre-sale for tickets runs through November 3, with general admission (first-come, first-served) opening November 15 at 10:00 AM. All seats are reserved at ¥9,900 (tax included).

![Budokan concert announcement](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F05%2F30111835%2F%25E2%2598%2585260528_budoookan_kokuchi_n.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Check Aooo's official channels and the ticket link (eplus.jp) for full details and T&Cs. This is a pivotal moment for the band—don't sleep on securing your copy or catching them live.
