Novel Core has unleashed an intense live video capturing one of their most explosive fan favorites, "Kami Sama Kidori," and it's exactly the kind of raw energy you'd expect from the band.

![Novel Core performing on stage](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F14151057%2F263551_Novel-Core%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%2586%25E3%2582%25A3%25E3%2582%25B9%25E3%2583%2588%25E5%2586%2599%25E7%259C%259F.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

"Kami Sama Kidori" has become a staple of Novel Core's live arsenal—a track that consistently ignites venues with ferocious band dynamics and relentless stage presence. The newly released footage captures the band's performance from their 2026 national tour finale at Toyosu PIT in June, shot entirely from an on-stage camera perspective. This unique vantage point showcases the symbiotic chaos between Novel Core, accompanying act THE WILL RABBITS, and the crowd below: fists pumping, bodies diving, heads thrashing in perfect synchronization. It's the kind of intimate-yet-explosive moment that defines a truly memorable live experience.

The video is culled from the band's live album *Perfectly Defective Tour 2026 (Live at Toyosu PIT 2026.6.26)*, which dropped on September 16 and contains seventeen tracks capturing the full setlist from that night. If you want the complete sonic document alongside the visual, the album is streaming across all major platforms.
