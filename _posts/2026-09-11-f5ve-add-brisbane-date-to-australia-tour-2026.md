The demand is real—f5ve's Australian fanbase just proved it in the most definitive way possible. Following the near-instant sellout of their London debut just weeks ago, the Japanese visual kei outfit has now added a third Australian date after their initial Melbourne and Sydney shows sold out within hours of tickets going live.

![f5ve group photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F10192043%2Ff5ve-group-icy-no-text.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those unfamiliar, f5ve are one of Japan's most visually striking contemporary acts, blending theatrical presentation with a rock-forward sound that's earned them a fervent international following. Their aesthetic—moody, intricate, and unapologetically theatrical—resonates deeply with Western visual kei audiences who've been craving the genre's elaborate stage presence and emotional intensity.

The new Brisbane show was greenlit after overwhelming fan requests flooded the band and local promoters. In a statement, member KAEDE expressed genuine excitement about meeting Australian fans for the first time, promising a show that would "wrap audiences in our music and transport them to a dream world."

**Here are the complete details:**

**f5ve + the deep: f4n m33t australia**

- **October 7, 2026** – The Triffid (Brisbane)
- **October 8, 2026** – Prince Bandroom (Melbourne)
- **October 9, 2026** – Manning Bar (Sydney)

All shows are all-ages. Tickets for the Brisbane date went on sale September 11 at 11:00 JST. The Melbourne and Sydney dates are already sold out, so if you're in Queensland, don't sleep on this one.

This tour continues f5ve's impressive international momentum. Their ability to sell out venues across continents speaks to how hungry Western audiences are for authentic visual kei experiences—especially from acts that treat the genre not as nostalgia, but as a living, evolving art form.

Head to f5ve's official website, X, Instagram, and YouTube channel for ticket links and the latest tour updates.
