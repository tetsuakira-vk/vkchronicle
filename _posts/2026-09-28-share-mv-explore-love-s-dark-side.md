Visual Kei outfit 黒蜜 (Kuromitsu) are diving into the more complicated corners of romance with their latest music video drop. The "飼い猫" (Kaiineko / Pet Cat) visual, taken from their debut full-length album "純愛フルコース" (Junai Full Course), arrives as the band continues their sprawling 47-prefecture one-man tour under the same "純愛" (Pure Love) banner.

For those new to 黒蜜: the band has built their identity around exploring "pure love" from every conceivable angle—and "飼い猫" marks a significant step into murkier emotional territory. The track tells the story of a deeply unequal attachment: wanting someone so badly that you swallow your own feelings, choosing to stay close even when you know their heart belongs elsewhere. There's a kind of aching acceptance in that surrender, a willingness to exist in someone else's orbit despite the pain.

The song's title carries real weight. A pet cat relies entirely on its owner for survival and affection, with no guarantee that devotion will be reciprocated equally. It's a perfect metaphor for the obsession and self-sacrifice the band explores here—and crucially, 黒蜟 aren't romanticizing it. Instead, they're asking harder questions: *Is being reciprocated the only measure of love? Does happiness have to be the end goal of genuine devotion?*

This latest track shows the band expanding beyond surface-level romance narratives into the possession, dependency, and sometimes toxic undercurrents that exist within even sincere feelings. It's mature territory for a band building a discography around a single emotional concept.

The "純愛" tour wraps December 19th at Tokyo's Shibuya-AX, so if you've been following 黒蜜's journey through Japanese venues, now's the moment to lock in your final show before the year closes. Given the tour's scale and momentum, tickets are expected to move fast.

Check 黒蜜's official channels for ticket availability, streaming links, and the full "飼い猫" video to experience the visual storytelling firsthand.
