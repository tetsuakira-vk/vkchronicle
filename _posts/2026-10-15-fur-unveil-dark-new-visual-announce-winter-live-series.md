FUR just delivered a birthday surprise that's got the Visual Kei community buzzing. On October 15th, vocalist Cion's birthday, the five-piece took the stage at SHIBUYA RING to debut a striking new visual direction that marks a significant aesthetic shift for the band.

Gone is the ornate glamour FUR has cultivated since their formation. In its place: a unified, darker energy. The band appeared cloaked in jet-black enamel outfits, posed against a blood-red moon—an image that immediately sparks curiosity about their winter mini-album *LUNAFUR*, set to drop later this year. If this is a preview of what's coming, expectations are through the roof.

![FUR's new visual featuring five members in black enamel outfits](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2FphCion-1-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For Western fans still getting to know FUR, this Tokyo-based outfit has been quietly building momentum with an almost relentless touring schedule. Their first single *GLOSS* dropped in August, and they've already established a beloved recurring series called "LOUNGE"—intimate, themed events that showcase a more playful, experimental side of the band.

The band wasted no time announcing what's next. December 14th will see *FUR ViVi BIRTHDAY 黒服限定ONEMAN 【ViLLAiNS】* (an all-black-dress dress code event) at Shibuya Star Lounge. Fanclub members can access the fastest ticket draw through Ticket Dive, with general admission opening shortly after.

![FUR performing at ViVi birthday show](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph20261215_FUR_ViViBD-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Beyond that, FUR's beloved "LOUNGE" series returns with two installments at Nishi-Ogikubo BETTYROOM: November 13th's *LOUNGE #9 -BELATED UNDEAD-* and December 26th's *LOUNGE #10 -X'MAS-*, both with tiered ticket pricing and creative costume themes.

Want to stay on top of FUR's upcoming moves? Head to their official site at **fur-official.com**, follow their activity through their YouTube channel and X account, and secure tickets through the links above. This is a band firing on all cylinders—don't sleep on them.
