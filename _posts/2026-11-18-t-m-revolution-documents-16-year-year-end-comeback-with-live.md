After a 16-year hiatus, T.M.Revolution's legendary year-end countdown live series made its triumphant return in 2025—and now fans worldwide can relive the electric energy with an official home video release.

The T.M.R. YEAR COUNT DOWN PARTY LIVE REVOLUTION REMIX XIV (commonly known as "Iyakan") dropped five sold-out shows across Japan in late 2025. This December 23rd, the final performance from Zepp DiverCity in Tokyo arrives on Blu-ray and DVD, capturing the raw intensity that had devoted fans waiting more than a decade-and-a-half for this beloved annual tradition to resume.

For those less familiar with T.M.Revolution, the project of vocalist Takanori Nishikawa has been a cornerstone of the Japanese music landscape since 1996. The artist's breakthrough hit "HOT LIMIT" has racked up over 100 million streams, while recent solo work under the Nishikawa name—including the viral "FREEDOM" and his appearance on "THE FIRST TAKE"—has proven his enduring relevance. Beyond music, he's balked traditional boundaries, working as an actor, voice actor, and event curator for his hometown's annual INAZUMA festival in Shiga Prefecture.

![T.M.Revolution limited edition live Blu-ray packaging](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Ftm_kannzen-580x816.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The Iyakan XIV setlist spans career essentials—from "HOT LIMIT" and "Preserved Roses" to deeper cuts like "Web of Night" and "緋の砂" (Aka no Suna). The complete production limited edition bundles a live audio CD and photobook with an exclusive pin badge (¥12,500 / ~$85 USD). Standard Blu-ray editions run ¥7,000 (~$48 USD), while initial production runs include photobooks as bonus material.

Looking ahead, Nishikawa is running dual revival tours throughout 2026–2027 titled "REWIND OF CLOUD NINE" and "REWIND OF Joker Type 2," hitting major halls across Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya, and beyond. He'll also headline the New Year Party '27 at Nippon Budokan on January 1st.

For pre-orders and ticketing details, visit the official T.M.Revolution website at tm-revolution.com. Follow his latest moves on X (@TMR15) and Instagram (@takanori9nishikawa).
