<h2>October 2026 in Review: Autumn Brings Farewells, Comebacks, and Career Milestones Across the Visual Kei Scene</h2>

<p>October reminded us why Visual Kei endures: a month of bittersweet goodbyes mixed with triumphant returns, genre-pushing new music, and the kind of career moments that define a scene's history.</p>
