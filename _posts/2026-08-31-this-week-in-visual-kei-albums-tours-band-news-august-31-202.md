It's been a packed week in the Visual Kei sphere, and honestly, it feels like every corner of the scene has something brewing. From surprise collaborations to major tour announcements, this has been the kind of stretch that reminds us why we stay glued to VK news.

Let's start with the new music. Zilqy came swinging with the fastest track yet from their debut album "Birth of Riot"—heavy Visual Kei fans are already losing it. Meanwhile, 04 Limited Sazabys made their return with the 'VIEW' EP, and they've gone full creative mode with a tattoo artist collab that's got everyone talking. Over in the anime tie-in department, go!go!vanillas locked down a placement on the Tokyo Revengers soundtrack with "Trickster," proving Visual Kei's ongoing crossover appeal.

Tour season is absolutely heating up. Zeke Deux just packed September through November with a proper heavyweight schedule—birthday tour, European dates, and Halloween shows all on the slate. Z CLEAR, fresh off welcoming new drummer Yū, launched their "序曲" tour and the energy around that lineup shift is undeniable. machine brought the cyberpunk heat to the finale of their REVIVE SPIRITS TOUR, leaving fans thoroughly melted.

The live events and streaming side saw some cool moments too. kurayamisaka's Zepp DiverCity show is going nationwide with a live stream, so if you missed out on tickets, you've got another shot. Ambition Label threw a two-day celebration for their 2nd anniversary, and WHISPER OUT LOUD announced KEYS IN PURPLE vol.4—the hybrid rock event calendar is stacked. 黒蜜 won over the internet by making a fan's guitar wish come true during a surprise live collab on YouTube, which is exactly the kind of heartwarming VK content we live for.

Radio and broadcast deserve mentions too: Mishisan hosted their second Niconico Radio Show featuring DASEIN and ν[NEU], while V-Entai TV kept the Nagoya VK scene on the map with their DIFFERENT:ACCOUNT broadcast special.

What's making this week feel special isn't just the volume—it's the variety. We're seeing established bands refresh their lineups and hit the road with renewed energy, newer acts pushing sonic boundaries on debut projects, and fan interaction moments that remind us why community matters in this scene.

Keep your eyes peeled next week; the touring calendar only gets busier from here.
