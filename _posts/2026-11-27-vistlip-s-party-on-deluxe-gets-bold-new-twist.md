vistlip is turning their signature hosted event into a full-scale collaborative chaos machine, and they're documenting every awkward, brilliant moment along the way.

The Tokyo Visual Kei outfit has announced that all band members will participate in guest appearances across every act performing at their December 4th showcase—and unlike previous iterations of [Party On], they're pre-announcing this commitment by visiting each participating band to negotiate the details. It's a refreshingly transparent approach for a scene that typically thrives on surprise moments.

![vistlip members in discussion](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F27190816%2Fvistlip_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E5%2586%2592-1024x682.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Guitarist Kai's visit to Azavana's one-man live and bassist Rui's trip to meet newly-arrived Arulukan drummer Akira spawned surprise collaborations—including an unexpected twist when Arulukan's fresh recruit caused a last-minute pivot from originally-planned guest Tohya. These ambush negotiations are being filmed and uploaded to vistlip's official YouTube channel, capturing the band's characteristic politeness clashing hilariously with their audacious plans.

The remaining approaches to MUCC, GUMMY, and Royz have already been filmed and are pending release. What makes this especially compelling is vistlip's positioning: they're navigating dynamics with both established senior acts and rising names within the same show, so their approach to each will likely reveal plenty about band politics and genuine friendships within Tokyo's Visual Kei community.

**Event Details:**

**vistlip presents [Party On] -Deluxe-**
December 4, 2026 | CLUB CITTA', Kanagawa
Open 14:00 / Start 15:00
Lineup: Azavana, Arulukan, GUMMY, MUCC, Royz
Tickets: ¥6,500 advance / ¥7,500 door (plus drink charge)

vistlip also has two additional year-end events lined up: The Nightmare Before Merry Bell (December 20, Osaka BIGCAT) and Merry Bell 2026 (December 23, Tokyo Spotify O-EAST).

Tickets are on sale now via e-plus. For the latest updates on cross-band collaborations and member appearances, check vistlip's official YouTube channel and social media.
