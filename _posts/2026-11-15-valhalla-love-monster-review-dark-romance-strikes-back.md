VALHALLA's return to the spotlight with LOVE・MONSTER marks a significant moment for the symphonic Visual Kei veterans. After years of relative quiet, this single announces not just a comeback, but a deliberate artistic statement—one that leans into the band's greatest strength: the collision of orchestral grandeur with raw emotional darkness.

The title itself signals intention. The dual-language blend of "LOVE" and "MONSTER" (rendered with that characteristic・midpoint) immediately positions this as thematic territory VALHALLA knows intimately: the duality of passion and destruction, beauty and terror. It's a formula that defined their most iconic work, and there's genuine relief in hearing them return to it now, in 2026, when much of Visual Kei has fragmented into niche subcategories.

What makes LOVE・MONSTER compelling is its refusal to chase contemporary trends. This is unapologetically symphonic—strings swell, arrangements breathe with orchestral weight, and the production feels intentionally lush rather than compressed. In an era dominated by trap-influenced beats and digital minimalism, VALHALLA's commitment to analogue grandeur feels almost rebellious. The vocal delivery appears characteristically theatrical, with the kind of dynamic range that separates genuine Visual Kei from diluted alternatives.

There's a measured confidence here that suggests the band didn't rush this single to market. Every flourish feels deliberate, every dramatic peak earned rather than manufactured. Whether LOVE・MONSTER serves as a prelude to a full album remains unclear, but as a standalone statement, it functions perfectly: a reintroduction to longtime fans and a stark reminder of why VALHALLA mattered in the first place.

This is essential listening for anyone invested in Visual Kei's symphonic lineage.

RECOMMENDATION: Immediate stream for established fans; essential discovery for newcomers to orchestral Visual Kei.

**Rating: 8/10**
