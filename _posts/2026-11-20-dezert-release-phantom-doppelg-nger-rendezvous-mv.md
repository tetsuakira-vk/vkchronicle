DEZERT has unveiled a playful new music video for "亡霊ドッペルランデヴー" (Phantom Doppelgänger Rendezvous), a standout track from their October major-label second album. The visual proves why this song has already become a live favorite — it's bursting with creative energy and shows a band that refuses to take themselves too seriously.

Drummer SORA returned to direct and edit the clip, following his work on the equally inventive "TOXIC" video. The result is a visual journey that kicks off with retro anime aesthetics before shifting and evolving in sync with the song's dynamic structure. It's clear that SORA knows exactly where this track hits hardest, and working with bandmates who share his vision means every beat lands with intention. What really sets this apart is the spontaneity captured on set — when SORA had an idea, the band embraced it immediately, creating something that radiates playfulness throughout.

![DEZERT band photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F20144225%2FA%25E5%2586%2599DEZERT%25E3%2580%258CDEZERT%25E3%2580%258D.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

This marks the third consecutive month of new DEZERT visuals, following "TOXIC" in September and "生きてゆこうね" (Let's Keep Living) in October. For those hungry for more from their latest album, the band's YouTube channel is packed with additional MVs for "無修正。" (Uncensored), "火花" (Sparks), and "音楽" (Music), plus official live videos and lyric videos for "Welcome To The End," "My Unhappy Life," and "オーディナリー" (Ordinary). It's a comprehensive visual feast.

DEZERT is also announcing their 15th Anniversary Fan Club Limited "Acoustic & Talk" tour for January 2027, hitting eight cities across Japan. Dates include stops in Kashiwa, Mito, Fukuoka, Takamatsu, Yokohama, Saitama, Niigata, and Yonago, with most venues opening at 4:30 PM.

Head to DEZERT's official YouTube channel and social media to watch "亡霊ドッペルランデヴー" and explore their complete video archive. This is essential viewing for anyone following the band's creative evolution.
