After years away from the spotlight, Jubaku Hyourei has officially announced its return—and this time, the project is transitioning from a special side venture into a legitimate, fully-fledged band.

For those unfamiliar with the project, Jubaku Hyourei (literally "Curse-Bound Spirit") emerged as DazzlingBAD's experimental offshoot, blending the theatrical darkness and compositional ambition the Visual Kei scene is known for with a distinctly conceptual approach. The project initially operated in a limited capacity, maintaining an air of mystery that drew devoted followers. Now, with this official revival announcement, the band is ready to commit to a more permanent presence within the scene.

The decision to evolve from a special project into a standalone entity signals serious creative intent. This kind of transition typically means fans can expect more frequent activity: studio releases, regular live performances, and deeper engagement with the Visual Kei community at large. It's a move that suggests the members have found their footing artistically and are confident enough to pursue this vision on a grander scale.

For Western fans, this is particularly exciting news. While Japanese Visual Kei acts don't always prioritize international touring or distribution, a band's official establishment often precedes expanded reach. As more fans discover the project's aesthetic and sound, there's genuine potential for Jubaku Hyourei to gain traction beyond Japan's borders.

The announcement itself marks a turning point—a moment where a band stops existing in the margins and steps into the arena. Whether this leads to new material, live dates, or further surprises remains to be seen, but the momentum is clearly building.

For the latest updates, news, and release information, keep an eye on the band's official channels. This is one revival worth watching closely.
