After nearly two decades of crafting avant-garde Visual Kei soundscapes, THE MICRO HEAD 4N'S are preparing to close one of Japan's most experimental chapters. The band has unveiled their final artist photograph—a poignant image capturing both their journey and the emotions of what's to come—as they countdown to their last performance this December.

![THE MICRO HEAD 4N'S final artist lineup photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2FphKEKE-1-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those discovering the band for the first time: THE MICRO HEAD 4N'S built their reputation on genre-defying electronics, ethereal vocals from KEKE, and intricate production courtesy of guitarist/programmer kazuya—supported by bassist ZERO and drummer TSUKASA. Their sound occupies a unique space in Visual Kei, blending industrial textures with melodic sensibility in ways that influenced countless acts within the underground scene.

The band's final live, titled "The End from the Beginning," will take place on **December 18, 2026, at Spotify O-EAST** in Tokyo. The ¥10,000 all-standing show promises to be a reflective sendoff befitting such an influential act. Every attendee will receive a free remastered version of "声なきスマイル" (Koe Naki Smile)—a fitting memento of their legacy.

![THE MICRO HEAD 4N'S guitarist kazuya](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fphkazuya-2-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Before that farewell, the band has scheduled several events: a two-man show with アルルカン on November 7 at Kawasaki Serbian Night, a members-only celebration on November 8 in Shibuya featuring both a live set and meet-and-greet opportunities, and a makeup show for their "HELLO MY CLONE" tour on November 28 in Osaka.

Tickets for the December finale are on sale now through Pia. For complete event details, lineup information, and to follow their final chapter, head directly to the band's official site at themicrohead4ns.jp. This is a moment Visual Kei history in the making—don't miss it.
