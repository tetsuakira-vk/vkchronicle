A Visual Kei legend is celebrating a major milestone. After two decades away from the music scene, kei-jigowatt9 stepped back into the spotlight in September 2025 as a solo artist—and now, barely a year into his return, he's marking the occasion with a new single that carries real emotional weight.

For those unfamiliar with the name: kei-jigowatt9 is the vocalist who powered through the VK underground in the late 1990s and 2000s, fronting bands including Vinett, Eliphas Levi, and Rätsel. After a lengthy hiatus from the industry, he made an unexpected return last fall, this time operating entirely as a one-man operation. Everything—songwriting, composition, arrangement, mixing, mastering, and artwork—comes from him alone.

**HAPPY BIRTHDAY**, his fifth digital single, drops today (October 10, 2026) across all major streaming platforms. The track centers on a concept he's wanted to explore for years: celebrating birthdays and the often-overlooked beauty of wishing happiness onto another person. It's a deceptively simple idea that, in his hands, becomes something more introspective—an invitation to reflect on those quiet, precious moments we share with people who matter.

The single lands as both a thank-you to supporters who've stuck with him through this unexpected solo chapter and a genuine 1st-anniversary celebration. There's something distinctly generous about that gesture: rather than retreat into nostalgia or self-commemoration, he's created something meant to resonate with listeners' own lives and loved ones.

What makes this release particularly compelling is kei-jigowatt9's hands-on approach. Having spent decades within the collaborative chaos of band dynamics, his solo era allows for an unfiltered artistic vision—every vocal inflection, every production choice reflects his creative intent alone.

**Where to listen:** Stream "HAPPY BIRTHDAY" now on Apple Music, Spotify, Amazon Music, AWA, and other platforms via his official link.
