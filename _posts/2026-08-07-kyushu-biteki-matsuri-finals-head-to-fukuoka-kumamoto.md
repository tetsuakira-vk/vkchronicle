The final chapter of one of Kyushu's most anticipated Visual Kei events is about to unfold. The Kyushu Biteki Matsuri (Kyushu Aesthetic Festival) is touching down in Fukuoka and Kumamoto this August, bringing together a carefully curated roster of established and emerging VK acts for what promises to be a landmark summer weekend.

Biteki events have carved out a reputation for bringing together diverse sounds within the Visual Kei spectrum—everything from melodic rock to experimental territory—and this two-city finale maintains that aesthetic. Whether you're familiar with established names like Sadie's Courreges and 東京花嫁 (Tokyo Hanayome) or discovering emerging acts for the first time, the lineup balances accessibility with edge.

**Fukuoka Dates & Details**

The Fukuoka leg kicks off on August 8th at Fukuoka DRUM Be-1. Doors open at 1:30 PM with performances starting at 2:00 PM. Advance tickets are ¥6,000 plus a ¥1,000 drink charge; day-of pricing is ¥7,000. The Fukuoka bill includes エトセトラ (Etcetera), 生憎の雨。(あいにくのあめ), K-STYLE, Sadie's Courreges, obje, 東京花嫁, MERALOA, Shiki, Opium, and BUGS, with ぼんど餅フン太朗 handling hosting duties and an opening performance.

![Kyushu Biteki Matsuri promotional flyer](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2FS__28893214-580x802.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

**Kumamoto Show & Important Info**

The festival continues August 9th at Kumamoto B.9 V1 (2:00 PM doors / 2:30 PM start). This date features a slightly adjusted lineup: エトセトラ, 生憎の雨。, Pulowzillia, Sadie's Courreges, 東京花嫋, MERALOA, 瘋癲, and ぼんど餅フン太朗. Ticket pricing mirrors Fukuoka at ¥6,000 advance or ¥7,000 day-of, both plus drink charges.

**Note for prospective attendees:** The Kumamoto region experienced seismic activity prior to this event announcement. The organizers have established a refund process for affected fans—check their official TimeTree and the provided form for eligibility and details.

Both venues allow one re-entry, and entry will be seated by order number from your ticket receipt. Grab your tickets now while stock lasts.

Secure your spot through the official TiGET ticketing links and bookmark the official Kyushu Biteki Matsuri website and TimeTree for any last-minute lineup or venue updates.
