Plastic Tree vocalist and guitarist Arimura Ryutaro is back with a major solo statement. After nearly eight years away from solo work, he's just released *Personal Works Collection 2022–2026 "Demo #3"* through DAIZAWA RECORDS, the label imprint of UK.PROJECT. This isn't just a quickie passion project—it's a carefully crafted album that showcases Arimura's evolving artistic vision outside his main band.

The production lineup reads like a Visual Kei dream team. Alongside Arimura's own arrangements, the album features contributions from Mishima Sohei (cinema staff) and Kobayashi Yusuke (THE NOVEMBERS / THE SPELLBOUND), both respected figures in the underground scene. Engineer Tsugihara Fumiaaki—known for his work with Ling Tosite Shigure, ALI, and Wurts—returned to collaborate with Arimura, lending each track a crystalline clarity while preserving their unique character.

![Album artwork for Demo #3](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F25152614%2Fdemo3_ryutaro_A-724x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The lead single, "Yurimizu," arrived with a striking music video that captures Arimura's distinctive world. According to the artist, this track evolved organically through live performances with his support musicians, gradually transforming until it found its final form on the album. The accompanying visual is as carefully considered as the track itself—a gorgeous, immersive piece that refuses to look away.

For those catching him in Tokyo, DAIZAWA RECORDS is screening a special spot on the Shinjuku Uniqlo Vision billboard from today through November 17th.

The album comes in two formats: an initial edition (¥3,500 + tax) with eight main tracks plus six acoustic versions, and a standard edition (¥2,800 + tax) featuring the eight core songs. Tracks range from the intricate instrumental work of "Genkeikōdo" to the haunting "Reizōko," with titles that hint at Arimura's poetic approach to songwriting.

Head to UK.PROJECT's official channels and DAIZAWA RECORDS to order your copy—this is essential listening for anyone invested in where Plastic Tree's main creative force is taking his solo artistry.
