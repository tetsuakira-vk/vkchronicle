IQ99 is gearing up to celebrate seven years of existence with a landmark one-man live that promises to blend emotion, magic, and the relentless positivity the group has become known for. The event, titled **TWINKLE KILLER**, arrives this November at one of Tokyo's most respected mid-sized venues.

For those unfamiliar with IQ99, the five-member collective has carved out a distinctive niche as what they describe themselves: the "ultimate happy entertainment idol group." Rather than chasing the conventional idol sound, IQ99 positions themselves as emotional healers—delivering entertainment that sits close to everyday life and reaches listeners on a deeper level. It's a philosophy that's resonated strongly enough to sustain them through multiple one-man shows over the past few years.

![IQ99 band members promotional photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%2586%25E3%2582%25A3%25E3%2582%25B9%25E3%2583%2388%25E5%2586%2599%25E7%259C%259F-8-580x387.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The group—comprising members Aisen Mii, Matsuyama Goma, Strong Zero, Nekozu An, and Koba Babīwi—returns to live performance after a year away with **TWINKLE KILLER** as their latest mission statement. The show's dual theme of emotion and magic suggests IQ99 are doubling down on the introspective, transformative energy that defines their act.

**Event Details:**

- **Date:** November 18, 2026 (Wednesday)
- **Venue:** Ebisu LIQUIDROOM, Tokyo
- **Doors:** 5:30 PM / Start: 6:30 PM
- **Tickets:** General advance ¥3,000 / Day-of ¥4,000 (plus drink charge). Tiered pre-sale options range from ¥5,000–¥50,000
- **On sale:** August 19, 2026 at 8:00 PM via LivePocket

If you've been following IQ99's journey or are curious about a group that treats idol culture as a vehicle for genuine emotional connection, this one-man is unmissable. Head over to their official website and ticket portal to secure your spot and stay tuned to their social channels for setlist announcements and updates.
