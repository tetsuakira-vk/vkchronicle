Psycho le Cému's seek is welcoming guitarist HIZAKI onto the 38th installment of his ongoing Nico Nico live radio talk show "seekのFish Dance Hour" (seek's Fish Dance Hour), airing October 21st at 7 PM JST. The program promises another engaging deep-dive into visual kei culture and artistry.

HIZAKI brings formidable credentials to the mic. Best known for his work with the symphonic metal outfit Versailles and the more progressive Jupiter project, HIZAKI has carved out a distinctive niche through his technical guitar work and compositional vision. His appearance marks a natural fit for seek's consistently impressive guest roster, which has included everyone from label head KISAKI to gothic metal pioneers La'cryma Christi.

The show's format remains unchanged: seek hosts alongside Seiichi Hoshiko (credited as the show's "opinion keeper"), creating a conversational space to examine visual kei's aesthetics, industry mechanics, and artistic evolution. With 37 prior episodes under its belt, the program has developed a reputation for frank, insider-level discussion that resonates with dedicated Western fans seeking unfiltered perspectives on the scene.

This particular episode lands at an interesting moment for both guests' projects. While both Versailles and Jupiter have maintained active schedules over recent years, appearances on platforms like this offer opportunities for behind-the-scenes storytelling and reflection that studio albums alone cannot capture.

The broadcast will initially stream free on Nico Nico, though viewers should note that partway through the program transitions to members-only access via the club Zy. channel (330 yen monthly subscription). First month is free for new members. The membership tier unlocks access to artist interviews, behind-the-scenes content, and priority ticketing for stylish wave events.

To tune in, head to the club Zy. channel on Nico Nico at the broadcast time. If you're already following Psycho le Cému or Versailles' official channels, both bands typically promote such appearances directly to their fanbases. HIZAKI's official social media accounts may also carry reminders closer to air date.
