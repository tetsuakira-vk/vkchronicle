Emerging Visual Kei outfit Kuromitsu are making waves with fresh visuals and new material as they barrel through their ambitious 47-prefecture nationwide tour. The four-piece has just unveiled updated artist photography and announced an upcoming music video for "飼い猫," a standout track from their debut full-length *純愛フルコース* (Pure Love Full Course).

For those new to the band, Kuromitsu centers their identity around the concept of "pure love" (純愛). The lineup consists of vocalist 乃亜, guitarist 龍一郎, bassist SAM, and drummer 伊織. Since launching in May 2026, they've been relentless in building momentum—their TikTok presence alone has generated over 200,000 views for the track "君のハーフツインを食べたい" (I Want to Eat Your Half-Twin), signaling genuine traction with younger audiences discovering VK through social platforms.

![Updated Kuromitsu band photography](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fphkuromitsu_f-a4_20260902_ol_kaineko-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The *Pure Love Full Course* album explores multifaceted definitions of love through sonic storytelling. "飼い猫" sits firmly within that thematic framework, and the incoming MV should provide viewers with a visual companion that crystallizes whatever "pure love" means through Kuromitsu's lens. Expect something visually striking that honors the band's emerging aesthetic.

![Kuromitsu 47-prefecture tour poster](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fphkuromitsu_f-a4_20260902_ol_47tour-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Their exhaustive tour schedule runs through December 2026, wrapping with a final show on December 19th at Tokyo's SHIBUYA REX—which will also double as vocalist 乃亜's birthday celebration. If you're in Japan or planning to be, venues span from Hokkaido down to Okinawa, making this a genuine grassroots campaign to connect with VK fans across the entire nation.
