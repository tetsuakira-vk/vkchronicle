The host of LOOP ASH is bringing back his laid-back Niconico radio show for a third round, and this time he's assembling an eclectic guest lineup that spans multiple corners of the visual kei underground.

Mishsan's ongoing talk program "Opa ❤️ Opa ❤️ Mishsan desu 👀" returns on **December 22nd at 7 PM JST** with two confirmed guests: Miku from Antic-Café and Ricky, who appears across both DASEIN and RIDER CHIPS. For those unfamiliar with the roster, Miku brings the elegant gothic-pop sensibilities of Antic-Café's coffee-shop aesthetic, while Ricky's dual involvement in the heavier DASEIN sound and the alternative RIDER CHIPS project offers a broader spectrum of discussion.

This marks Ricky's third appearance on the program—a testament to both his personality and the chemistry he's built with Mishsan. Miku joins as a fresh face this time around. The show maintains its casual vibe, trading formal interviews for genuine conversation, making it a solid pick for fans wanting to hear artists discuss their work in a more relaxed setting.

**A heads-up on access:** The broadcast will transition partway through to a subscriber-only stream. Club Zy's channel membership (¥450/month, with the first month free) unlocks the full experience, including archived original artist videos, exclusive interviews, and bonus radio content. Members also gain early ticket access to the stylish wave event series.

The first two episodes have already built momentum—Ricky appeared in both, alongside ALiBi's Kai in the premiere and ν[NEU]/DearLily's Hiro in episode two. These aren't scripted segments; they're genuinely conversational windows into how these musicians think and connect with one another across different projects.
