MUCC frontman Tatsurou is taking his streaming talk show in a bolder direction. The reimagined "Hojikuri ROCKS!" — a deep-dive conversation series backed by Maverick DC Group — debuts November 22nd with an unexpected first guest: KIZU vocalist Raimu.

![Hojikuri ROCKS promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F19170802%2F%25E3%2581%25BB%25E3%2581%2598%25E3%2581%258F%25E3%2582%258AROCKS%25E2%2599%25AF81_%25E5%2591%258A%25E7%259F%25A5%25E7%2594%25BB%25E5%2583%258F-1024x576.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The original "Ijikuri ROCKS!" format — where Tatsurou and guests would dissect music news with a playful twist — built a solid following through collaborations with the likes of Hyde and Kirito. But the relaunch pivots toward something more intimate: one-on-one interviews that dig deeper into an artist's true self and unfiltered thoughts.

The gimmick that drives the conversation? Seven cards emblazoned with the characters of "Hojikuri Talk" (掘じくりトーク). Questions flow from Tatsurou to guest, guest to Tatsurou, plus viewer submissions and insider intel from crew members. Which card gets flipped next remains a live surprise — a tension that only real-time streaming can deliver. NoGoD's leader, formerly the assistant MC, shifts into a "voice of heaven" role as the show's new anchor.

![KIZU's Raimu](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F19170733%2F%25E3%2582%25AD%25E3%2582%25BA%25E6%259D%25A5%25E5%25A4%25A2_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E5%2586%2599-683x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Raimu's appearance carries particular weight: KIZU announced earlier this year that the band will enter a partial hiatus following their second Budokan headline show on February 27th, 2027. With that milestone looming, Tatsurou — who has built relationships across the Japanese rock scene — will probe what's behind Raimu's decision and the emotions tied to this pivotal moment. Expect the reverse direction too: Raimu asking Tatsurou questions of his own.

The second half of the broadcast steps into members-only territory on Niconico Live. Here, Tatsurou and Raimu will cook takoyaki together while continuing their conversation — a deliberately relaxed setting designed to strip away formality and reveal a looser, more candid version of the guest.

**Stream "Hojikuri ROCKS! #81" live on November 22nd at 7:00 PM JST via YouTube and Niconico Live. The first half is free for all viewers; the full broadcast requires a Maverick Channel membership on Niconico.**
