BILLY BOO has just revealed plans for an ambitious nationwide tour kicking off next February, and the announcement itself was theatrical enough to match their aesthetic. The band unveiled "BILLY BOO presents 'THE LOST PARADE'" during the opening night of their GAMBIT tour 2026 in Osaka—a sold-out show that set the tone perfectly for what's coming.

![BILLY BOO band photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F06164416%2FBILLY-BOO_ArtistPhoto_2606-1024x768.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those still getting familiar with BILLY BOO: the outfit represents the contemporary Visual Kei scene with theatrical energy and visual precision. They've been building momentum steadily, and this tour marks a significant step—each show will feature guest artists, suggesting they're positioning THE LOST PARADE as something more festival-like than a traditional headlining run.

The tour spans five cities across Japan from February through late April 2027. Doors open at 16:30 with performances starting at 17:00 for all dates. Here's where you can catch them:

**February 13** — Osaka Museum (Osaka)  
**February 23** — Drum Son (Fukuoka)  
**March 13** — ell.FITS ALL (Aichi)  
**March 27** — Sendai Macana (Miyagi)  
**April 29** — Spotify O-West (Tokyo)

![Tour poster](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F06164421%2FBILLYBOO_tour-766x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The guest lineup hasn't been announced yet, so there's genuine intrigue around who'll share the stage with them. This strategic mystery should keep fans engaged as we approach 2027.

Beyond this tour, BILLY BOO are also confirmed for Merry Rock Parade 2026, so they're shaping up to be a fixture on Japan's festival circuit this year and into next.

For ticket information and full details, check BILLY BOO's official channels and social media—they'll be dropping guest announcements and ticketing info soon. Mark your calendars.
