A bold new initiative is challenging the boundaries between Visual Kei and mainstream J-rock, and it's launching this October in Tokyo. The MASKED organization—known for their "V-kei is freedom" ethos since 2025—has announced **MASKED BEYOND vol.1**, a specially curated bill designed to create unexpected chemistry between two distinctly different rock scenes.

The event takes place **October 13 (Tuesday) at clubasia in Tokyo**, with doors opening at 5:30 PM and the show starting at 6:00 PM. Four bands will perform: **Arurucan** and **RAZOR** representing the Visual Kei side, alongside **Azami** and **KALA** from the broader J-rock world. The concept isn't just about mixing genres for novelty—it's about pushing each scene forward by fostering genuine cross-pollination between their music, aesthetics, and fanbases.

![Visual Kei band performing on stage](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F18130240%2F1-724x1024.png&w=800&output=jpg)

The MASKED organization has already proven this model works. Their 2026 spring events across three Shibuya venues (Spotify O-EAST, O-WEST, and duo MUSIC EXCHANGE) sold out both days, demonstrating genuine appetite among fans to experience Visual Kei and J-rock artists sharing the same stage space.

MASKED BEYOND positions itself beyond simple genre fusion. By embracing the concept of "BEYOND"—moving forward, transcending boundaries—the event frames participating bands as trailblazers willing to venture into unfamiliar territory. What kind of creative sparks will ignite when these four acts converge? That's the real draw here.

**Ticket Information:**
- **Pre-sale A (Lottery):** August 17–26 via Ticket Pia (max 4 tickets per person)
- **Pre-sale B (Lottery):** September 7–16 via Ticket Pia & eplus
- **General Sale:** September 26 onwards via Ticket Pia & eplus
- **Pricing:** ¥4,800 (advance); ¥5,300 (day-of), plus facility charge

For full details and official updates, visit the MASKED website at www.masked-v.com. If you're in Tokyo and curious about where Visual Kei heads next, this is unmissable.
