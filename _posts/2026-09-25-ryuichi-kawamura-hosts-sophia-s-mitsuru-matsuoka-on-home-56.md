A poignant reunion is unfolding in the world of Visual Kei's most influential vocalists. Ryuichi Kawamura, the iconic frontman of LUNA SEA, is welcoming Mitsuru Matsuoka of SOPHIA as his guest for the 56th installment of his ongoing "Ryuichi Kawamura Live 2026: Home" streaming concert series. Scheduled for October 16, 2026, this intimate digital performance promises to be something truly special—a full-circle moment spanning three decades of mutual respect between two legends.

![Ryuichi Kawamura](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F26000044%2F01__Ryuichi-Kawamura_solo-682x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those unfamiliar, LUNA SEA remains one of Visual Kei's most enduring and respected acts, with Kawamura's commanding presence and vocal prowess defining an era of the movement. SOPHIA, meanwhile, emerged from Osaka's underground indie scene to become a pillar of emotional, poetic Visual Kei rock. The connection between these two artists runs deeper than most realize.

In a heartfelt message, Matsuoka revealed that Kawamura was instrumental in SOPHIA's early trajectory—inviting the then-unknown frontman to his home before the band had even fully formed, listening to rough demos, and offering encouragement that would sustain Matsuoka through decades of struggle. "Your eyes are good. Your voice is good. Come to Tokyo soon," Kawamura reportedly told him.

![Mitsuru Matsuoka](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F26000044%2F01_Mitsuru-Matsuoka_solo-683x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Now, more than thirty years later, Matsuoka will perform alongside that same "charisma" he once found so dazzling. The setlist will include a collaborative rendition of "BEAT," a signature solo hit from Kawamura's peak era—chosen by Matsuoka himself. Beyond the music, expect rare behind-the-scenes anecdotes from SOPHIA's formation and an exploration of their unique artistic bond.

The broadcast originates from Kawamura's newly renovated Home studio space (NEW KAMOME st) and will set a new record for streaming duration. This is a pre-recorded broadcast, not a live transmission, so don't expect real-time interaction.

**Stream Details:**
- **Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Time:** 7:30 PM open / 8:00 PM start (scheduled to end 9:30 PM)
- **Ticket Price:** ¥5,000 (tax included)
- **Archive Availability:** Until October 21, 11:59 PM
- **Platform:** eplus STREAMING+

Ticket purchase and full event details are available through the official eplus ticketing system. If you've followed either LUNA SEA or SOPHIA through the years, this is an unmissable glimpse into two legends celebrating a relationship that shaped Visual Kei history.
