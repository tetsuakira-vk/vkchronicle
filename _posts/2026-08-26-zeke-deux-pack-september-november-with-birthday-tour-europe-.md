Zeke Deux are heading into an absolutely stacked final quarter of 2026, and it's shaping up to be their most ambitious season yet. Between member birthday celebrations, a European mini-tour, and their fifth anniversary special, the Tokyo-based band is giving fans plenty of reasons to clear their calendars.

For those newly discovering Zeke Deux, they're a melodic Visual Kei outfit known for blending theatrical aesthetics with emotionally resonant songwriting—think dramatic hooks, gorgeous melodies, and the kind of visual presentation that demands to be seen live.

The festivities kick off in September with the "Haruka & TOSSY Birthday Tour ~Virgo to Libra~," a four-city, six-show run celebrating guitarist Haruka and drummer TOSSY. The real showstopper arrives on September 17th at Tokyo's Ikebukuro BlackHole, where all five members will perform across three separate session bands—and notably, former member Maya will join them for an additional session. This isn't your typical setlist-and-go show; it's structured as a special event that promises something genuinely different from their usual live format.

![Zeke Deux band promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2FHPSDf5Za8AAsQrK-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Debuting at the tour's September 12th opener in Sapporo, the new single "Rose Quartz" will be available as a limited venue-only physical release. The track encapsulates everything Zeke Deux does well: dramatic production, pristine melodies, and heart-grabbing songwriting. Expect it to become a live staple immediately.

The band also debuts new costumes during the September tour—a visual refresh that long-time supporters will want to witness firsthand. October brings an exciting pivot: the "Mini Europe Tour" sweeps through Germany (Berlin), Czech Republic (Prague), and Austria (Vienna), giving continental fans a rare chance to catch them outside Japan. Upon returning home, they'll hit the Halloween Tour circuit across major cities before culminating everything with their fifth anniversary 2MAN show on November 11th at Ikebukuro BlackHole alongside Redo (featuring Ruiza).

This is the kind of touring schedule that separates serious bands from the rest. Three distinct live experiences, international dates, and a landmark anniversary all compressed into ten weeks.

For tickets, dates, and further information, head to Zeke Deux's official website at zekedeux.jp, where the full 2026–2027 schedule is listed. Follow their X account (@ZekeD_Official) for last-minute updates and behind-the-scenes content as the whirlwind begins.
