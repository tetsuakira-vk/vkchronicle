Two decades into a solo career that's redefined Visual Kei's relationship with genre and tradition, ASAGI is pulling out all the stops for a milestone that deserves nothing less than a full-scale artistic statement.

The artist—best known for pioneering the fusion of traditional Japanese aesthetics with avant-garde rock—has announced three releases arriving December 9th, 2026, that collectively represent the most ambitious project of his two-decade solo journey. It's not just a repackaging exercise; it's a philosophical statement split cleanly down the middle between East and West.

![ASAGI 20th Anniversary Album Cover](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fafly2-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

**Stab ～Part II～** captures the Japanese side of ASAGI's artistic DNA: a 15-track exploration of wabi-sabi philosophy, folklore, and the transience of existence rendered through music. The limited edition adds the "Asagi Butterfly" music video and behind-the-scenes footage. **Testament**, by contrast, presents his Western-facing vision across two CDs and 20 tracks, climaxing with an ambitious new suite, "Mad King Cantata Zero" (both prelude and postlude versions). Both releases arrive in limited editions with DVDs and standard CD-only versions at ¥5,500 and ¥4,400 respectively (roughly $37–40 USD).

For completionists, **Opera Omnia** is a staggering six-disc compendium: four CDs containing all tracks from his 1997 debut through both new albums, plus DVD and Blu-ray collecting 15 previous music videos and two new behind-the-scenes releases. At ¥22,000 ($150 USD), it's a serious collector's item.

What elevates this beyond a vanity project is the 55 guest musicians involved. ASAGI's assembled a who's-who spanning LUNA SEA, DIR EN GREY, Versailles, SIAM SHADE, GALNERYUS, and crossover names like Heather Paauwe, representing rock, classical, traditional Japanese instruments, and everything between.

The live campaign is equally elaborate: a winter-into-spring national tour structured as 2-day runs where night one explores the "Japanese" setlist and night two tackles the "Western" vision. Dates kick off October 31st in Akita and run through at least February 2027, with special events including a zoo excursion and exclusive meet-and-greets for album buyers.

ASAGI's YouTube channels have already dropped both new music videos with English subtitles—a deliberate nod to his international fanbase.

For tickets, venue information, and streaming links, check ASAGI's official channels directly. This is the kind of 20-year statement that rewards paying attention.
