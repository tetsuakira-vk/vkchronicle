A rare collaborative performance is headed to Nagoya this April, bringing together two distinctive acts for an intimate evening of music. VINYL unplugged and Shinjuku Shinon-kai's Itaya Yu will share the stage for "A Midspring Night's Dream" — a two-man show designed with early Sunday departure times in mind for accessibility.

![VINYL unplugged promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2FVa-580x435.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

VINYL unplugged, the project of Fukui Yoshishi and Suzuki Shin, has carved out a niche for atmospheric, introspective soundscapes within the VK underground. Known for their layered guitar work and experimental approach to composition, the duo brings a meditative quality to their performances. Shinjuku Shinon-kai, led by vocalist and guitarist Itaya Yu, represents the more visceral end of the spectrum—delivering energetic, emotionally charged sets that showcase expressive vocal delivery and dynamic band chemistry.

This collaboration signals mutual respect between two artists working in distinctly different spaces. Each will perform a full 60-minute set, allowing for deep exploration of their respective catalogs without feeling rushed.

![Itaya Yu promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fitaya-580x677.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

**Event Details:**

The show takes place on **Sunday, April 25, 2027** at Rock Bar UK in Nagoya's Hoshigaoka district. Doors open at 5:00 PM with performances starting at 5:30 PM—an early start that means you'll be wrapped up by evening. Ticket reservations open **December 5, 2026 at 8:00 PM** exclusively through TIGET. Pre-sale pricing is ¥4,500; day-of tickets are ¥5,000 plus one drink.

For full details and to secure your spot, visit the TIGET reservation page or check Rock Bar UK's official schedule. Follow VINYL unplugged on X and Itaya Yu's official blog for any updates leading up to the performance.
