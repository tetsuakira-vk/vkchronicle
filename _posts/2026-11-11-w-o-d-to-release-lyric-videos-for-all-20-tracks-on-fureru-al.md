w.o.d. is pulling out all the stops for their upcoming double album, and fans are in for a treat with an innovative rollout strategy centered around Instagram.

The Tokyo-based Visual Kei act has announced that all 20 tracks from their forthcoming album *Fureru* (Touch) will receive dedicated lyric videos, rolling out simultaneously on November 18, 2026 — the album's official release date. But here's the creative twist: each song gets its own dedicated Instagram account within the "Fureru_files" folder structure currently visible on w.o.d.'s official Instagram. These aren't just standard lyric videos either. Each track-specific account will host handwritten lyrics, exclusive audio snippets you won't hear anywhere else, and exclusive photographs. It's a brilliant way to deepen fan engagement and give people a reason to explore beyond the album itself.

![w.o.d. band members](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F06%2F10140121%2F%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%2586%25E3%2582%25A3%25E3%2582%25B9%25E3%2583%2881%25E5%2586%2599%25E7%259C%259F-3.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

*Fureru* marks w.o.d.'s first full-length album in roughly two years and arrives as a double album spanning 20 songs. The band is releasing it across three formats: a limited CD box set with a Blu-ray (containing live footage from their "I SEE LOVE Tour" and "LOVE BUZZ Tour," plus music videos and 2025–2026 documentary footage) for ¥12,000; a standard 2CD first pressing for ¥4,500; and a premium 180-gram vinyl double LP for ¥11,000.

![Album artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F08165457%2F%25E5%25AE%258C%25E7%2594%259F%25E7%2594%25A3CD_%25E3%2582%25B8%25E3%2583%25A3%25E3%2582%25B1%25E5%2586%2599.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The tracklist spans two discs and includes previously released singles like "ふれる" (Fureru) and "Feelin'" alongside new material like "DIVE," "TOKYO CALLING," "NON-FICTION," and "YOLO."

To celebrate the release, w.o.d. is hosting a one-day pop-up event on November 23 at an undisclosed venue. Anyone presenting a physical copy of *Fureru* gains free entry to exclusive displays and limited merchandise. They're also launching a nationwide 19-venue one-man tour titled "ONE-MAN TOUR 2027 'Fureru'" — fanclub pre-orders are currently open.
