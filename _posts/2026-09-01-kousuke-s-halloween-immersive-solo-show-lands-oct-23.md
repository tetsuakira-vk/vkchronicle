If you've been wondering what happened to Kousuke after his recent transition to solo activity, the answer is more intriguing than a simple career pivot—he's reimagining the live experience itself.

Now performing primarily as a drummer for other acts while building his own artistic vision, Kousuke is stepping into uncharted territory with what he's calling an "immersive solo performance" (イマーシブワンマン). Rather than a conventional one-man show, this is a narrative-driven event where the story continues even during set changes, crafting what the artist describes as a "surreal world that doesn't exist in reality."

The concept is genuinely ambitious: from the moment you enter the venue until you leave, you'll be completely immersed in Kousuke's constructed universe. This isn't just about musical performance—it's about environmental storytelling and sustained atmosphere.

**The show details:**

Taking place on **October 23rd at Sugamo Shishiou** (巣鴨獅子王), the event is titled "Navigation Log: Return × Night Banquet × Paradise" (航海日誌『回帰✖️夜宴✖️楽園』). Doors open at 5:40 PM with performances beginning at 6:10 PM.

Three ticket tiers are available:

- **A Ticket** (advance, ¥4,000+) – includes a charm lottery (魔除けくじ)
- **B Ticket** (day of, ¥4,500+) – includes charm lottery
- **C Ticket** (free, advance and day of)

Entry is prioritized by ticket type, with A-ticket holders entering first based on order numbers.

Given the Halloween timing, Kousuke explicitly encourages attendees to come in costume if the spirit moves you—though this is equally welcoming to anyone looking for something genuinely different to do on a Friday night. The artist's emphasis on immersion and world-building suggests this won't be your typical venue experience.

For ticket information and further details, check out the Club Zy venue website and keep an eye on Kousuke's official channels for any last-minute announcements.
