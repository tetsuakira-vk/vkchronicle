To celebrate the milestone, the band has lined up a special release event and high-touch meet-and-greet session for fans eager to connect with the new outfit.

While details on venue and exact timings remain sparse from the announcement alone, this kind of grassroots fan engagement is a classic hallmark of how emerging VK acts build their initial fanbase in Japan. It's a direct, personal way to reach supporters who've been following their journey from the ground up — particularly important for a debut single that marks the official beginning of KEY TO LIT's recorded legacy.

The announcement also includes a full reveal of the coupling track titles, something that's often saved for release day but here gets pushed into the spotlight early. This suggests the label is confident enough in the material to let fans digest the tracklist in advance and build anticipation. For Western followers who may not have encountered KEY TO LIT yet, now's an ideal moment to dig into what they're about — their sound, their visual aesthetic, and whether they align with your taste in modern Japanese rock.

Debut singles carry weight in the Visual Kei world. They're the statement piece, the introduction that shapes how the fanbase perceives a band's identity and direction. Whether KEY TO LIT are chasing symphonic darkness, aggressive guitar work, or something more experimental, this release will tell the story.
