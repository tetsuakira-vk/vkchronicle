Visual Kei fans tuning into Niconico on September 29th are in for a treat—Mishisan from LOOP ASH is back with the second installment of "Opa❤️Opa❤️Mishisan Desu👀," her casual radio talk show, and this time she's bringing some stellar guests into the mix.

For those unfamiliar with Mishisan, she's been making waves within the VK community as a member of LOOP ASH, a band known for their energetic presence and growing fanbase. Her radio series feels like catching up with a friend over drinks—relaxed, genuine, and full of unexpected tangents that make for great listening.

This second episode welcomes Ricky from the hard-hitting unit DASEIN and RIDER CHIPS, alongside Hiro from ν[NEU] and DearLily. If you caught the first broadcast featuring Ricky and Kai from ALiBi, you'll know what to expect: candid conversation, insider stories, and that special chemistry that only happens when musicians from the same scene get together and let loose. The dynamic between these artists promises to be equally entertaining.

The broadcast kicks off at 7 PM JST on Tuesday, September 29th. Here's the catch: the show will transition partway through to club Zy.'s member-exclusive stream. Beyond this radio series, you'll gain access to artist-exclusive videos, special interviews, behind-the-scenes photography, and priority ticket booking for events like stylish wave.

Club Zy.'s member perks include a solid archive of past Niconico broadcasts, making it a worthwhile investment if you're serious about staying connected to Japan's underground music scene. The first episode already proved this format works—intimate enough to feel exclusive, but energetic enough to keep the momentum rolling.
