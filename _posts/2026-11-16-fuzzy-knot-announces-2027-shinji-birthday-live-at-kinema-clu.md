fuzzy knot is bringing back their beloved birthday celebration for the fourth consecutive year. The unit project—born from the creative partnership between sid guitarist Shinji and versatile vocalist Takayuki Tazawa (known for his work with Rayflower and solo endeavors)—will host a special concert honoring Shinji's birthday on February 8, 2027, at Tokyo's Kinema Club.

For those unfamiliar with fuzzy knot, the project represents an intriguing collision of styles: Shinji's razor-sharp guitar work from sid's visual kei foundation meets Tazawa's dynamic vocal presence across multiple musical contexts. The result is a unit that captures something genuinely unique—equal parts celebration and musical exploration.

![fuzzy knot promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Ffuzzyfly-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The announcement emphasizes that this annual gathering has become a cherished tradition—a day where the stage and audience merge into one unified experience, wrapped in music and smiles. There's genuine warmth in how the organizers frame it: not just a concert, but a collective moment of gratitude toward the fanbase that sustains both Shinji and Tazawa's respective projects.

**Event Details:**
- **Date:** Monday, February 8, 2027
- **Venue:** Kinema Club, Tokyo
- **Time:** OPEN 18:15 / START 19:00
- **Ticket Price:** ¥7,000 (standing, excluding drink charge; ages 4+)

Ticket sales launch today via fuzzy knot's official site with a presale window running from November 16 (12:00 PM) through December 1 (11:59 PM). Winners will be announced December 4, with payment due by December 7.

If you've caught fuzzy knot's previous birthday shows or you're curious about what this unit brings to the table, mark your calendar now. Head to the official fuzzy knot website for full details and ticket access, and follow their social channels for any last-minute announcements as the date approaches.
