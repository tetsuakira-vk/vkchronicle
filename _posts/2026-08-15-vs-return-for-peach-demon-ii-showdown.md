One year after their sold-out clash, two of Visual Kei's most compelling forces are meeting head-to-head once again. 鬼ヶ島 (Onigashima) and 浅葱 (Asanegi) have announced a return engagement this December, and if last year's event is any indication, this one won't last long on the ticket market.

The rivalry between these acts carries real weight. 鬼ヶ島's frontman Kou-Oni and 浅葱 share label history, which adds genuine stakes to their face-offs. These aren't just two bands splitting a bill—they're locked in a direct confrontation that fans have come to anticipate. The success of 2025's "Peach Demon Legend Story" (桃鬼伝来物語) proved there's serious appetite for watching them square off, so the decision to make this an annual tradition feels inevitable.

"Peach Demon Legend Story II" takes place December 12, 2026, at EDGE Ikebukuro. Doors open at 4:00 PM with the show starting at 4:30 PM. Advance ticket pricing is ¥6,000, with day-of tickets at ¥6,500 (plus a separate drink charge).

The ticket structure follows a tiered release: preorders (Tier A) open September 9 at noon and run through September 23 at 11:59 PM, with payment due between September 25–28. General sale (Tier B) begins October 3 at 10:00 AM. Both tiers allow up to four tickets per person. Entry order follows the tiering system, with day-of tickets admitted last.

This is the kind of event where Japanese Visual Kei fans clear their schedules months in advance. For Western supporters following the scene, this represents a rare opportunity to witness a genuine inter-band dynamic that's become central to how 鬼ヶ島 and 浅葱 build their respective narratives.

Tickets are available through the official Eplus link—grab them early if you're planning to attend or interested in supporting either band's next chapter. Check both acts' official channels and social media for any additional announcements closer to the date.
