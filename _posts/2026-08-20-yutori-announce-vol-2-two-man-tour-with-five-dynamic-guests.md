yutori is stepping up their game this fall with an ambitious two-man tour that promises to showcase the band alongside some of Japan's most explosive live acts. The Tokyo-based four-piece—fronted by vocalist and guitarist Sato Kotoko—has announced the full lineup for their "yutori 2MAN TOUR 2026 「対 vol.2」," a sequel to their successful 2024 tour concept. What's particularly exciting is how yutori has curated each guest band specifically for their destructive live presence and artistic synergy.

![yutori band photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F20162811%2F3_yutori_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E3%2583%2586%25E3%2582%25A3%25E3%2582%25B9%25E3%2583%2588%25E5%2586%2592%25E7%259C%259F-1024x750.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The tour kicks off October 25 at Kyoto's KYOTO MUSE with NELKE, followed by stops in Sendai (November 2, MACANA with プッシュプルポット), Nagoya (November 13, THE BOTTOM LINE with TETORA), Osaka (November 21, BIGCAT with PEDRO), and Tokyo (November 29, Spotify O-EAST with ハンブレッダーズ). Each date represents a deliberate pairing—yutori's statement emphasizes they've chosen only bands capable of "breaking" their stage presence and creating entirely new live experiences for everyone in the room.

Alongside the tour announcement comes exciting news: yutori is releasing their new single "秘密" (Secret) on August 26. The track explores intimacy and forbidden desire from a woman's perspective—specifically, the tension of harboring romantic secrets your partner will never know. It's a four-piece rock anthem built on driving energy and emotional authenticity, balancing the raw vulnerability of its lyrical subject with urgent instrumentation.

![Himitsu single artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F20162926%2F2_8%25E6%259C%258826%25E6%2597%25A5%25E9%2585%258D%25E4%25BF%25A1Sg%25E3%2580%258C%25E7%25A7%2598%25E5%25AF%2586%25E3%2580%258D%25E3%2582%25B8%25E3%2583%25A3%25E3%2583%2583%25E3%2582%25B1%25E5%2586%2599-1024x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The artwork features Sato with her index finger to her lips—a visual echo of the song's central conceit. Pre-save starts August 20, and anyone who registers by August 25 at 11:59 PM will receive an exclusive smartphone wallpaper.
