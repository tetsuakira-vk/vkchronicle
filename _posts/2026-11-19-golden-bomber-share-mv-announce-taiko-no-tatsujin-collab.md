Golden Bomber are ramping up the theatrics with a brand-new double A-side single and an unexpected arcade game partnership that's sure to turn heads across both the Visual Kei and rhythm game communities.

For those unfamiliar, Golden Bomber are Japan's self-described "Visual Kei air band"—a deliberately provocative group fronted by the charismatic Shō Kinryu (vocals) alongside Toyoshige Kiyaraki (guitar), Atsushi Utahiroba (bass), and Kenji Tarubishi (drums). They've built a reputation for blending theatrical aesthetics, satirical humor, and genuinely catchy songwriting into a package that refuses easy categorization.

On December 2nd, 2026, the band will release their dual-sided single **"病名をつけてくれ / 残高ゼロ"** (literally "Give Me a Diagnosis / Zero Balance"). The lead track's accompanying music video dropped today, and it's a masterclass in tonal contrasts: Shō expresses genuine distress about the nature of his own emotional struggles—themes of procrastination and emotional numbness that hit close to home for anyone—while his three bandmates dance with reckless abandon around him. It's simultaneously sincere and absurd, exactly what Golden Bomber do best.

The single's jacket artwork embraces gothic aesthetics, with the three instrumentalists grinning wickedly while apparently restraining a pleading Shō in a composition that's both unsettling and undeniably pop-forward.

But there's more. To celebrate Taiko no Tatsujin's 25th anniversary, both album tracks will drop into arcade cabinets starting December 2nd, making Golden Bomber the latest artists to receive the rhythm game treatment. The band will also headline a live stage performance at Sunshine City in Ikebukuro on December 4th at 6:30 PM—part of Namco's larger Namja Town 30th anniversary celebration.

The ¥2,000 CD edition includes karaoke versions of both tracks plus a serial code for exclusive MV behind-the-scenes footage.

For full details on ticket drawings for the Ikebukuro stage event (entries open November 20th through 29th), and to stay updated on additional collaboration announcements, head to Golden Bomber's official website and social channels across X, Instagram, and TikTok.
