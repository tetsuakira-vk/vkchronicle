Visual Kei outfit Ta-rot is charging forward with a strategic revival campaign, beginning with the September 5th digital reissue of two reimagined classics: "PSYCHO" and "Tsurushi-nin" (The Hanged Man).

For those unfamiliar with Ta-rot, the band has carved out a distinctive corner of the V-kei underground with their darkly theatrical approach and conceptual storytelling. This release marks an intriguing pivot—revisiting and re-recording material from their catalogue, suggesting a band confident enough to recontextualize their own archive for both longtime followers and newcomers.

The digital single bundles both tracks together at ¥700 and becomes available September 5th at 12:00 JST via their official store. This isn't a one-off nostalgia play, either. Ta-rot has mapped out an aggressive autumn rollout: expect new original material dropping throughout October and November on consecutive months. That kind of momentum signals the band is operating at full creative throttle.

The announcement also confirms what might be the year's most intriguing Visual Kei live event: Ta-rot's one-man show "Yugama Senbanger" on December 5th at Ueno Oto Yokocho. Ticket sales begin September 15th. Given the band's reputation for immersive, concept-driven performances, this headlining slot should deliver the theatrical intensity fans expect.

The rollout strategy here is worth noting—Ta-rot is layering announcements methodically, ensuring sustained attention through the final quarter of 2026. This isn't accidental. Each piece of news (revival release, new originals, live confirmation) feeds into the next, building narrative momentum.

If you're tracking Ta-rot's next chapter, start here: grab the digital single from their official Mental-C store, then lock your calendar for the December live. Follow their official X account for the promised October and November announcements—the band has made clear this campaign is still unfolding.
