What if your favorite band showed up to teach you guitar in person? That's exactly what happened for one lucky fan of 黒蜜 (Kuromitsu), the pure-hearted Visual Kei outfit built on the concept of "genuine love" connecting artist and listener.

黒蜜 have launched an interactive YouTube series titled "I Want to [Do Something] for You," designed to grant wishes submitted by their fanbase. The project kicked off to commemorate the release of their track "君のハーフツインを食べたい" (I Want to Eat Your Half Twin), inviting supporters to submit heartfelt requests they'd love to see fulfilled. Rather than generic celebrity favors, the band commits to granting genuine moments of connection through music itself.

![黒蜜 band members performing with fan](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F%25E9%25BB%2592%25E8%259C%259C0828%25E3%2582%25B5%25E3%2583%25A0%25E3%2583%258D_-580x324.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

This time around, a fan requested guitar lessons—and 黒蜜 delivered in the most heartwarming way possible. The participant had spent weeks rehearsing one of the band's own compositions, determined to master it before the shoot. When band members appeared as a surprise, the fan got to perform the finished song alongside them in real-time.

The exchange captured something special: watching dedicated fans pour effort into understanding their music wasn't lost on 黒蜜. The members visibly lit up, recognizing that this wasn't just about ticking a box on a wish list. By sharing musical space with someone who'd invested real time learning their art, the band exemplified the "pure love" ethos that defines their project—genuine human moments built through the universal language of sound.
