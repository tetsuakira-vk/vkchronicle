What a week to be alive in the Visual Kei sphere. November gave us everything from explosive tour closures to surprise comebacks, and honestly, the energy has been *unmatched*.

Let's start with the live circuit, because it's been absolutely popping off. Zetsubou Kinema wrapped their first tour in explosive fashion with a Tokyo finale, cementing themselves as serious contenders in the theatrical rock space. The Nagoya scene contingent showed up hard, and the reports we're getting suggest this was one of those shows that'll define a band's early chapter. Meanwhile, T.M.Revolution proved that 16 years between "Year-End" installments is nothing when you've got the goods—their new live Blu-ray/DVD is a full-circle moment that tracks the icon's enduring pull.

On the radio and digital media front, Mishsan's third Niconico Radio outing brought together the powerhouse roster of Antic-Café and DASEIN, with LOOP ASH rounding out what sounds like an unmissable lineup for anyone streaming that night. And MUCC's Tatsurou flexed his talk-show muscles again, linking up with KIZU's Raimu for what we're hearing was a genuinely compelling live-stream conversation. These behind-the-scenes moments matter more than people realize.

The release calendar was stacked. Kizu delivered what critics are calling their peak work with "Gokuraku Yori Gokujou no Ame"—this isn't just an album, it's a full world-building flex that reminds us why they're one of the most conceptually ambitious acts in the VK game right now. DEZERT dropped their "Phantom Doppelgänger Rendezvous" MV, visually stunning and lyrically dense as expected. Golden Bomber, ever the chaotic good actors, surprised everyone with both a "病名をつけてくれ" MV *and* a Taiko no Tatsujin collaboration announcement. It's the kind of left-field move that keeps this scene unpredictable.

And then there's KEY TO LIT, the genuine wildcard story of the week. A new band announcing their debut single release event and fan meet? This is how legends start. The fact that they've already got enough momentum to organize a proper event before the single even drops speaks volumes about underground buzz.

This was the kind of week that reminds you why Visual Kei thrives—depth, range, and zero chill when it comes to creativity.

Watch the December schedule closely; if this week's pace holds, we're in for something special.
