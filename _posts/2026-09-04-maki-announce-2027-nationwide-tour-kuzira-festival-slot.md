Maki are keeping momentum rolling strong with a major two-pronged announcement: a fresh nationwide tour launching next January, plus the reveal of the final headliner for their annual festival.

![Maki Tour 2027 Flyer](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F04141451%2F%25E4%25BA%258C%25E7%259B%2583%25E5%258F%25A3%25E3%2583%2595%25E3%2583%25A9%25E3%2582%25A4%25E3%2583%25A4%25E3%2583%25BC-724x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

**The Tour**

Maki Tour 2027 "Niihaiguchi" kicks off January 29th in Fukuoka and spans six regional cities over the next three weeks. The dates hit Hiroshima, Takamatsu, Kanazawa, Sendai, and Sapporo—a deliberate move by the band to strengthen live house scenes across Japan's provinces. As they've stated, there's a real commitment here to meeting more fans face-to-face in intimate venue settings rather than always chasing the biggest stages.

Official presales open September 4th at 9 PM (closing September 23rd). Co-bill lineups for each stop will roll out later. Full venue and ticketing info can be found through their official channels.

**The Festival Slot**

KUZIRA have secured the final guest spot at Maki presents. Music Festival "Tenho" 2026 (November 22nd, Aichi). This is a big deal: they're the only act getting a back-to-back appearance, returning after last year's inaugural edition. Both bands share deep roots—same generation, same label, described as "comrades bound by wordless understanding." It's a real vote of confidence in KUZIRA's evolution and growth.

![Festival Lineup](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F04140651%2F%25E3%2583%25A1%25E3%2582%25A4%25E3%2583%25B3%25E3%2583%2593%25E3%2582%25B8%25E3%2583%25A5%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25AB_0904%25E6%259C%2580%25E7%25B5%2582%25E8%25A7%25A3%25E7%25A6%2581%25E4%25BB%25A5%25E9%2599%258D-819x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The festival also features FOMARE, KOTORI, and HEY-SMITH. Doors open 2:30 PM; tickets are ¥6,500 presale / ¥7,500 day-of (plus one drink charge). Presales for the final guest reveal go live September 4th at 9 PM through September 13th.

Maki's current album tour "Suankou" is still rolling through October as well, so plenty of opportunities to catch them live before 2027 kicks in.

Head to Maki's official site, X, and Instagram for complete tour and festival details, plus ticket links through the official vendors.
