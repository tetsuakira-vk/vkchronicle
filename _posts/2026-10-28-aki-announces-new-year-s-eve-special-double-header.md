AKi—the solo project of SID bassist Aki—is ringing in 2027 with a bang. The visual kei musician will headline a special double-bill concert on December 31st at Thunder Snake ATSUGI in Kanagawa, delivering back-to-back afternoon and evening sets to close out 2026 with maximum impact.

For Western fans unfamiliar with AKi's solo venture, it's a chance to experience the SID bassist's artistic identity outside the band's established sound. While SID continues their "Re:Quest" hall tour throughout 2026, AKi has carved out space for this parallel project, supported by guitarists Tappa and YOUSAY, plus drummer Shio (from MAQIA).

![AKi promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F1_a-580x410.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The "AKi LIVE 2026 New Year's Eve Special" kicks off at 14:30 and 17:30, with each show running approximately 90 minutes. Tickets cost ¥15,000 (roughly $100 USD) and are now available through AKi's official online salon, "Resonants," with pre-orders running through November 3rd.

What makes this show unmissable isn't just the music. Attendees will receive curated gifts hand-picked by AKi himself, exclusive access to film one song on their phones for social media, and a post-show meet-and-greet. If that weren't enough, every ticket holder gets a personalized New Year's digital message from AKi delivered on January 1st—a thoughtful touch that speaks to his connection with fans.

![AKi live performance](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2_a-580x410.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

This New Year's Eve finale arrives amid a whirlwind December schedule. AKi is simultaneously hosting a co-headlining show with DEZERT on December 19th, a fan appreciation event on December 20th—billed as a "Christmas Special"—while maintaining his bass duties in SID's ongoing hall tour. It's an ambitious closing stretch that demonstrates AKi's commitment to delivering maximum creative energy as 2026 winds down.

![AKi band lineup](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F3_a-580x410.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For tickets and full event details, head to AKi's official website at dangercrue.com/AKi/, or join the "Resonants" online salon for priority access. Follow the latest updates on AKi's official X account (@AKi_official_) and SID's main site (sid-web.info) for additional tour announcements.
