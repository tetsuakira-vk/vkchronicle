Sometimes a musician needs to step away to find their way back. That's the story behind syo-ji's debut solo EP—a stunning return to form that proves lightning can indeed strike twice.

A guitarist who built his reputation in the 2000s Visual Kei scene, syo-ji eventually hung up his ambitions and retreated to his hometown of Shizuoka, working the night shift in the city's entertainment district. Yet music never truly left his heart. That smoldering flame was reignited in 2015 when mahiro—a legendary figure who'd been following syo-ji's journey across multiple projects including Drunken Wisdom, FAME, and HybriD—reached out with a simple but transformative question: "Why not give Tokyo and music another shot?" The question was enough to send syo-ji packing for the capital.

For years, however, syo-ji remained creatively adrift until meeting THOGO and his gatchinko (authentic hardcore) label and band. That connection opened doors to collaborators who would reshape his artistic vision: vocalist mahiro, voice coach and songwriter Kawagami Jiro (KUSU KUSU), Murata Go (Neurotica), and vocalist maki. Working through these relationships, syo-ji made a profound realization: his music flourished not when created for himself, but for others.

![syo-ji visual kei guitarist](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fjk-580x580.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

*'La Vie en rose'* crystallizes this philosophy across five carefully crafted tracks. Each song was written specifically with its featured vocalist in mind. The title track channels classic Visual Kei sensibilities with mahiro delivering lyrics of gratitude and life affirmation. Murata Go tackles "黄昏のテキーラサンセット" (Twilight Tequila Sunset), a melancholic blues-rock meditation on existence. Kawagami Jiro brings explosive pop-punk energy to "FULL SWING LOSER," while maki's crystalline vocals grace the Christmas-themed ballad "神さまの贈りもの" (God's Gift)—a song originally composed for an orphanage's holiday celebration. The instrumental "Espoir" rounds out the collection.

Releasing November 25, 2026, *'La Vie en rose'* marks not just syo-ji's solo debut, but a philosophical statement about creativity fueled by generosity rather than ego.
