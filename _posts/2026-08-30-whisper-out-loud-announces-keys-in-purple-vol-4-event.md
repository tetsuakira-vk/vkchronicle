WHISPER OUT LOUD is taking control of their own stage with the announcement of KEYS IN PURPLE vol.4, a two-day event that showcases the Tokyo-based band's rising influence in the visual rock underground. The next-generation hybrid city rock outfit has carved out a distinct identity that bridges theatrical aesthetics with contemporary rock sensibilities, and this event gives fans a chance to experience their curated vision across two very different formats.

![WHISPER OUT LOUD band photo](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F30114514%2FWOL-Artistphoto-2025-1024x684.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

**Event Breakdown**

Day 1 (October 8, 2026) functions as a multi-band showcase, featuring four acts at Shibuya REX. The lineup includes Jellow Glow, WALTZ, MOREkrage (performing as a WOL band set), and of course WHISPER OUT LOUD themselves. Doors open at 6:30 PM with performances starting at 7:00 PM. Advance tickets are ¥3,500, with day-of pricing at ¥4,000 (plus a ¥1,000 drink charge). Food vendor Hell See will be on-site.

Day 2 (October 9, 2026) shifts into a full one-man show format, giving WHISPER OUT LOUD the entire night to command the venue. Same time and location — 6:30 PM doors, 7:00 PM start. Advance tickets run ¥4,000, with day-of at ¥4,500 (plus drink charge).

**How to Get Your Ticket**

Tickets are available through eplus, with a tiered priority system: two-day pass holders (with special bonuses) get first entry, followed by single-day ticket buyers, and then hand-sales. A two-day option is available if you want to experience both formats without separate purchases.

For fans eager to support WHISPER OUT LOUD's self-produced event, check their official channels and the eplus ticketing page (https://eplus.jp/sf/detail/4192710001) for all current information and updates as we approach October 2026.
