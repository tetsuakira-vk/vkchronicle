ViVaRush is pulling out all the stops for their 10th anniversary—and yes, they're literally going nonstop for an entire day.

The Tokyo-based Visual Kei outfit has announced an unprecedented 24-hour livestream challenge running from November 14th at 9:00 PM through November 15th at 9:00 PM JST. The goal? Drive ticket sales toward a complete sellout of their anniversary Grand Final show in January 2027.

For those unfamiliar with the band, ViVaRush has built a reputation for high-energy performances and playful, unpredictable band personalities—a combination that makes them perfect candidates for a marathon streaming event. Their chaotic charm is already legendary among Western VK fans who've caught them at international festivals or through clips circulating online.

The livestream will air on their official YouTube channel and promises a stacked lineup of content. Expect the band's signature lively banter, a cooking segment, spontaneous song composition, a cover challenge of the trending track "CheeChee ROSIER," and a special radio recording featuring Arlucan's bassist Shouhei as a guest. The band is also actively inviting musicians and fans to drop in during the broadcast—so participation from the wider Visual Kei community is actively encouraged.

The Grand Final itself takes place on January 11th, 2027 (a national holiday in Japan) at Spotify O-WEST in Tokyo. Doors open at 4:15 PM, show starts at 5:00 PM. Standard advance tickets are ¥5,000 (plus fees), though the band is also offering special "10th Anniversary Thank You" tickets at just ¥100 to make the event more accessible.

All-standing format, ages 6+. Advance ticket holders get picture tickets and priority access to merchandise after the show. Fair warning: if advance tickets sell out, no day-of tickets will be available.

Head to ViVaRush's official website at vivarush.jp for full details, or grab tickets through the links provided at their X account (@vivar_official). This is one anniversary celebration that demands your attention—whether you tune in for the full 24 hours or catch the highlights.
