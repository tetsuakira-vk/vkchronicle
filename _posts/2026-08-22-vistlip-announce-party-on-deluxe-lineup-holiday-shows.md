vistlip is kicking off the festive season with a stacked bill featuring some of the most exciting names in the visual kei scene right now.

The band will host their headlining event **[Party On] -Deluxe-** on December 4th at Club Citta' in Kawasaki, bringing together five carefully curated acts spanning peers, seniors, and rising talents. The lineup includes Azavana (who have previously invited vistlip to their own hosted events), アルルカン—who collaborated with the band on recording and shared the stage at their friend & fiend 2026 event—the powerhouse new girls' band GUMMY (who exploded onto the scene in January 2026), the eternally connected MUCC, and Royz, who are gearing up for their historic debut at Nippon Budokan.

![vistlip performing on stage](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F22192009%2Fvistlip_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E5%2586%2599-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Beyond the December showcase, vistlip has announced their traditional yuletide one-man shows across Japan's major cities. Osaka will host **The Nightmare Before Merry Bell** on December 20th at Big Cat, while Tokyo brings **Merry Bell 2026** to Shibuya Spotify O-EAST on December 23rd. Both events promise the band's signature theatrical energy during the holiday window.

Looking ahead to 2027, the band is celebrating birthdays in style. Vocalist Tohya will hold his birthday live on January 3rd at Shin-Yokohama New Side Beach!!, with drummer Satoshi taking the spotlight on January 10th at Shibuya Spotify O-WEST in Tokyo.

![Azavana](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F08%2F22192231%2FAzavana_%25E3%2582%25A2%25E3%2583%25BC%25E5%2586%2599-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

This is a defining moment for vistlip—the convergence of established and emerging visual kei acts signals real momentum within the community. Whether you're catching the all-star December event or one of the holiday shows, head directly to vistlip's official website for full details and ticket information.
