After a decade away from the stages of Taiwan and Hong Kong, INORAN—guitarist and founding member of Visual Kei legends LUNA SEA—delivered a pair of triumphant homecomings this September that proved the enduring power of his solo artistry across borders and time.

INORAN carved out rare space from LUNA SEA's relentless touring schedule to bring his "Jade & Pearl Far East Island Asia Tour 2026" to Taipei on September 11 and Hong Kong on September 13. For fans in both cities, these were the first INORAN solo performances since 2016—a decade-long drought that only intensified the electricity when he finally took the stage. The response was overwhelming; both venues erupted as soon as INORAN greeted the crowd with a simple "I'm home."

![INORAN performing in Asia](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F16164711%2F6E6A1499.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The setlist honored INORAN's ongoing "Determine" revival tour—a project that's been revisiting his 2007 material throughout 2025–2026. This Asia leg kicked off with the titular track before blending newer songs like "Diffused Reflection" and "Walk Along" from his latest album *Nirai Kanai -Rerecorded-* alongside heavier rockers such as "Awaking in Myself" and "Grace and Glory." The INORAN BAND, reuniting for the first time in roughly ten months, executed every moment with impeccable precision. Staples like "Beautiful Now" and "Thank You" felt as fresh as ever, anchored by the band's unshakeable groove.

![INORAN with his band on stage](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F16164711%2FDSC04307.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

What emerged across those two nights was a masterclass in how music transcends language. The energy between stage and floor built into something genuinely moving—a reminder that rock's visceral power remains universal.

Now INORAN turns his sights homeward. On **October 30, 2026**, he'll headline Zepp DiverCity Tokyo for the "Determine" tour's **Grand Final**, billed as "Naked Again." Doors open at 6:00 PM; show starts at 7:00 PM. General admission tickets are ¥8,800 (tax included) and go on sale **September 19, 2026 at 10:00 AM** via e-plus, Lawson Ticket, and Ticket Pia.

For more details, visit INORAN's official website and check ticket vendors directly for availability and regional options.
