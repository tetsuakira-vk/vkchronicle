Jupiter continues to cement their place as one of Visual Kei's most sophisticated and evolving acts. In a recent in-depth discussion, guitarist and primary creative force HIZAKI opened up about the band's artistic trajectory, their relationship with the Visual Kei scene, and what fans can expect moving forward—including a long-awaited return to Europe in 2027.

For those unfamiliar with Jupiter, the band emerged from the ashes of the legendary Versailles, maintaining that project's orchestral grandeur while carving their own identity. Since their formation, they've built a reputation for ambitious concept albums and theatrical live performances that honor Visual Kei's roots while pushing toward something more expansive and progressive.

HIZAKI discussed how Jupiter's sound has matured across their releases, emphasizing the band's commitment to blending symphonic elements with heavy rock foundations. This evolution reaches a new peak with their latest album, "The Pantheon ~ Age of Heroes," a concept record that draws inspiration from mythology and heroic narratives. Rather than rehashing tired tropes, the band uses these themes to explore deeper questions about power, legacy, and human ambition—all delivered through Jupiter's signature wall of orchestral arrangements and aggressive guitar work.

What's particularly refreshing about HIZAKI's comments is his candid reflection on Visual Kei itself. Rather than dismiss the scene's theatrical elements as dated, he frames them as essential to Jupiter's identity and artistic freedom. In a musical landscape increasingly dominated by accessibility metrics and algorithm-friendly aesthetics, Jupiter unapologetically leans into the spectacle and complexity that define the genre.

The announcement of a 2027 European tour is significant for Western fans who've watched Jupiter build an international following despite the logistical challenges of touring abroad. Europe has always been receptive to Visual Kei's theatrical and progressive elements, and Jupiter's return promises to be substantial.

Catch up on Jupiter's latest work and stay tuned for tour announcements by visiting their official channels and streaming platforms. European fans should mark their calendars—2027 is coming.
