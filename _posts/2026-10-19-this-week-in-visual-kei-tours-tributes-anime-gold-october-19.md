What a week for the VK faithful. Between emotional milestone shows, major tour announcements, and some genuinely exciting soundtrack news, October 19th proved that the scene is firing on all cylinders right now.

Let's start with the heavyweights. DEZERT absolutely dominated the news cycle with a two-pronged announcement: fresh photos dropped alongside confirmation of their 15th Anniversary Tour Final, making this a genuine career milestone moment for one of Visual Kei's most respected acts. They're also part of something massive—DEZERT, D'ESPAIRSRAY, LM.C, and MUCC just announced a joint anniversary live for 2026 that has fans practically vibrating. This is the kind of supergroup collab that doesn't happen often, and the nostalgia factor alone is going to be astronomical.

On the emotional side, Kαin and D≒SIRE came together for a deeply personal tribute show honoring a late friend. These intimate, heartfelt events remind us why live VK matters beyond the spectacle—sometimes it's about community and remembrance.

The tour circuit keeps spinning: GODLAND wrapped their "Darkland Tour 2026" at the iconic Zepp Shinjuku (with MORRIE in tow), Psycho le Cému closed out their revival tour with a Shibuya finale that fans are still processing, and Zera expanded their 2026 schedule with Halloween tour visuals that look appropriately sinister. XANVALA's Tatsumi also celebrated a birthday with a special hometown show in Hamamatsu—always a nice touch when bands do something personal for their members.

New music and releases keep arriving steady: eyed shared a teaser for "silence" with some genuinely electronic, forward-thinking visuals that suggest they're pushing their sound in interesting directions. Novel Core dropped a live video from their Toyosu finale, and PEOPLE 1 announced a live Blu-ray for December 2026 called 'MY HAVEN HEAVEN AFTER DARK' that's already on collector wishlists.

But here's the plot twist: SUGIZO just scored the OST for a dark machine anime with a December release confirmed. Seeing X JAPAN and LUNA SEA's legendary guitarist branch into anime soundtracks is a reminder that Visual Kei talent keeps expanding its reach beyond club and arena stages.

FUR, meanwhile, unveiled a darker new visual direction and kicked off a winter live series—always worth paying attention to emerging acts with this kind of calculated aesthetic evolution.

It's been a dense, rewarding week that hits every angle: legacy acts cementing their impact, newer bands pushing boundaries, and that unmistakable sense that something significant is brewing heading into 2026.

Next week, we'll be watching for any surprise announcements from the joint anniversary live participants—don't miss it.
