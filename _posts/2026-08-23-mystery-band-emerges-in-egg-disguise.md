A cryptic new act just landed on the Visual Kei radar, and the internet is already spiraling with theories. Meet タマタマ(仮)—a band so shrouded in mystery that even their artist photos consist entirely of... eggs.

Yes, you read that correctly. Every single band member is represented by an egg. No faces. No names. No profiles. Just ovoid shapes and mounting questions about what on earth is happening here.

![Mystery band members in egg form](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph%25E9%259B%2586%25E5%2590%25882-2-580x388.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The group's identity is being kept under lock and key—or perhaps, under shell. The only concrete details fans have right now are the band's temporary name (which translates to something akin to "Egg Egg") and a countdown timer set to hit zero on September 7, 2026 at 19:00 JST. At that moment, the full reveal is promised.

But here's where it gets even weirder: the band plans to drop a music video spot before their members are even identified. So you'll hear their sound before you learn who's behind it. It's either a stroke of genius or absolute chaos—possibly both.

![Band member silhouettes](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph%25E3%2583%25A1%25E3%2583%25B3%25E3%2583%2590%25E3%2583%25BC1-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The burning questions linger: Is the egg motif symbolic? A statement? Pure absurdist humor? Or did someone genuinely just decide to commit to the bit? The official announcement refuses to elaborate, leaving Western fans and Japanese audiences alike in a state of delightful bewilderment.

If you're the type who loves a good mystery—especially one wrapped in Visual Kei's signature theatrical packaging—this is absolutely worth your attention. Head over to **[タマタマ(仮) official X account](https://x.com/tamatamaoffcial)** to keep tabs on the countdown and prepare yourself for whatever September 7th has in store. Mark your calendars now.
