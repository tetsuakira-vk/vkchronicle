When machine took the stage at Yokohama ReNY β on August 15th, they didn't just play a concert—they rewired the collective consciousness of a sold-out crowd. The cyberrock unit, reunited after a decade of silence, has spent 2026 reminding audiences exactly why their fusion of industrial noise, psychedelic urgency, and raw rock catharsis remains dangerously potent.

For those unfamiliar: machine pairs HAKUEI (PENICILLIN, The Brow Beat) with guitarist Kiyoshi (hide with spread beaver, Lucy, MADBEAVERS) in a project that splits the difference between Nine Inch Nails aggression and Japanese gothic experimentation. Their 2025 comeback—marked by the best-of collection *revive spirits ultra best "DEAD STOCK TOYS 2025"* with all tracks newly recorded—has proven the reunion was no nostalgia play. This lineup, expanded to five members with seek (bass), CHARGEEEEEE... (drums), and COLA (manipulator), punches harder than ever.

![machine performs at Yokohama ReNY β finale](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph_52_3576-%25E3%2581%25AE%25E3%2582%25B3%25E3%2583%2594%25E3%2583%25BC-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The evening erupted with "Phantom of the End," HAKUEI's voice cutting through distorted guitars and frenzied drums like a blade through silk. From that point forward, machine methodically dismantled the audience's defenses. "D.D.T (2025)" delivered crystalline menace wrapped in stuttering synths. "DIS" channeled punk electricity through a cybernetic filter—Kiyoshi's call-and-response vocals with HAKUEI creating a hypnotic push-pull that left bodies involuntarily swaying.

![HAKUEI commands the crowd](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph_52_3869-%25E3%2581%25AE%25E3%2582%25B3%25E3%2583%2594%25E3%2583%25BC-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The setlist deepened into hypnotic territories. "DEPTH" submerged consciousness in dub-like atmospherics before "baby 999 xxx xx xxx xxxxxx xxxxxxxx xxxxxxx (2025)" snapped listeners awake with acidic guitar shriek and tribal percussion. "Ooh yeah♡" pivoted toward euphoria—a machine-style seduction built on bass seduction and HAKUEI's velvet provocation. The crowd mirrored seek's playful gestures, transformed into willing participants in a ritualistic celebration.

![Kiyoshi's guitar work cuts through the mix](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fph_52_0584-%25E3%2581%25AE%25E3%2582%25B3%25E3%2583%2594%25E3%2583%25BC-580x866.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

By the finale—a blazing run through "Baby Blood (2025)," "Hero (2025)," and "猿の惑星 (2025)"—the boundary between performer and audience had dissolved entirely. HAKUEI's bark of "Hallelujah, toward the light" synchronized fists into unified gestures of surrender.
