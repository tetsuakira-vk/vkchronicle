After a five-month hiatus from solo band performances, KIRITO returned to live action on November 14th to kick off one of the year's most anticipated tours. The KIRITO Tour 2026-2027 "OVERRIDE" marks a pivotal moment for the artist, spanning 12 dates across two calendar years and culminating in January 2027.

For those unfamiliar with KIRITO, the musician has built a reputation as a dynamic force in Japan's underground rock scene, balancing intricate compositional work with visceral live performance. Throughout 2026, KIRITO maintained an impressive pace: an acoustic series in July, a two-man tour with D'ESPAIRKRAY in August, a festival appearance in September, and a concurrent three-show run as Angelo (his collaborative project with NOCTURNAL BLOODLUST members). That relentless schedule underscores just how significant this new tour cycle is.

![KIRITO performing at YOKOHAMA Bay Hall](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F11%2F16155748%2FKIRITO1114_%25E5%25AF%25841-683x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The "OVERRIDE" banner—Japanese for "overwrite"—signals a deliberate artistic statement. Earlier this year, KIRITO released the mini-album *SHIFT*, which marked a stylistic turning point toward melodic sensibility after three records (*NEOSPIRAL*, *ALPHA*, *CROSS*) that heavily emphasized dense, aggressive soundscapes. The OVERRIDE tour doesn't simply promote *SHIFT*; instead, it recontextualizes KIRITO's entire catalog, blending the heavier material with the new melodic direction into a cohesive setlist that showcases genuine artistic evolution.

The opening night in Yokohama saw KIRITO perform 24 songs alongside a stellar support lineup: JOHN on guitar, Yu-taro (NOCTURNAL BLOODLUST) on additional guitar, Chiyu (SLAPSLY) on bass, and Natsu (NOCTURNAL BLOODLUST) on drums. Thematically, the performance conveyed messages about unbreakable bonds and self-determination—core threads running through KIRITO's work.

Between sets, KIRITO told the crowd: "Let's keep burning through the year-change together"—a rallying cry for fans as the tour stretches from November into January's finale at Tokyo's Spotify O-EAST.

**Tour dates run through January 10, 2027, hitting venues across Yokohama, Sendai, Sapporo, Fukuoka, Nagoya, and Osaka before the Tokyo finale.** For details, ticket availability, and venue information, check KIRITO's official channels and social media directly—your best resource for real-time updates as dates approach.
