What a *stacked* week for Visual Kei. From legacy acts hitting major anniversaries to emerging bands charting bold new directions, this seven days reminded us why the scene keeps evolving without losing its edge.

**The Anniversary Moment**

MUCC just kicked off their 30th anniversary campaign with the single "Love Letter," and honestly? It hits different when a band that helped define modern Visual Kei is still pushing forward with this much conviction. They've also locked in live dates, so mark your calendars if you've been sleeping on these legends.

**Tour Season is *Happening***

The tour announcements came fast and furious. GLAY launched their "EXOFIRE" arena tour and announced a Taiwan return—international domination remains their default mode. BILLY BOO staked their claim with "THE LOST PARADE" Tour 2027, while Kroi dropped a recording documentary alongside their own 2027 tour plans. NAZARE sat down for an interview about their Latin America expansion and six years of taking Visual Kei globally. And if you needed confirmation that the scene's footprint is massive: Mirai Kodai Gakudan just booked Kanadevia Hall for May 2027.

**The Releases**

PassCode marked their 10-year major debut with the "GENESIS" music video—a full circle moment for one of electronic Visual Kei's most consistent forces. XANVALA dropped their fourth album CRITICAL. Azavana wove five previous singles into their new track "ALIVE" (with an MV to match). Ta-rot unveiled "Good Night" with a bold sonic refresh. And speaking of anime crossovers: VIVARUSH landed the opening theme for Tasuketsu, proving Visual Kei's cultural reach extends way beyond the live house circuit.

**The Culture Moves**

Beyond music, the ecosystem is diversifying. DANGER CRUE launched a TikTok Live agency for emerging artists—smart money on where the next generation hangs out. 黒夢 teamed with NEEDLES for limited collab tracksuits, blending fashion and Visual Kei aesthetics. And Seek from Psycho le Cému hosted HIZUMI on "Fish Dance Hour" radio, because sometimes the best Visual Kei content happens in unplanned conversations.

**Heavy Hitters**

Damian Hamada's Creatures unleashed "Blood on Blood" during TOUR 666—a reminder that the heavier, more experimental corners of VK are thriving.

It's the kind of week where you could spend eight hours just catching up, which is exactly how we like it. Next week: watch for live reports and reaction breakdowns—word is some of these tour announcements are already sparking demand.
