Kansai-based quartet umbrella are gearing up for a double celebration in February 2027, with dedicated birthday live events for vocalist/guitarist Yui and drummer Sho. The band has built a reputation for their spatial alternative sound—a densely layered, atmospheric approach to guitar-driven rock that rewards close listening—and these shows promise to showcase both members in distinctly different contexts.

Yui's birthday celebration kicks off on February 18th at Kyoto's legendary venue Magnetik, with the one-man live "Amayatori - The Eccentrics of the Ancient Capital 5." This marks the fifth consecutive year the guitarist has hosted his birthday show at this intimate Kyoto institution, and there's something particularly fitting about Yui returning to his hometown to perform in a space he clearly cherishes. The venue's distinctive atmosphere has become inseparable from these annual events.

![Yui promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fyui-580x820.jpeg&w=800&output=jpg)

Three days later, on February 21st, Sho takes the spotlight at Tokyo's Ikebukuro Shotou with "Amayatori - Zarathustra." But here's where it gets interesting: rather than a straightforward celebration, this event features umbrella alongside three other acts—Gyakushimana Haguruma wo Kamen ga Warau, Watashi wa Ukabinagara Shizumu, and Wonder Kuudo. The twist? Sho handles drums for every single band on the bill. It's a clever way to let fans experience his playing style stripped from umbrella's signature sound, showcasing his versatility across different musical contexts.

![Sho promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fsyo-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Both events have pre-order lottery ticket sales beginning November 26th at 10 PM (JST), with general sales opening December 13th. Tickets are ¥5,000 advance / ¥5,500 day-of (plus drinks).

For full details on tickets and umbrella's broader 2026–2027 schedule—including their 17th anniversary one-man in March—head to their official site at xxumbrellaxx.com, or follow them on X (@umbrella_DATA) and Instagram (@umbrella_official_) for the latest updates.
