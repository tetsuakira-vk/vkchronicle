The energy at Zepp Haneda doesn't just simmer—it combusts. hide with Spread Beaver's "REPSYCLE THE REPSYCLED" tour hit Tokyo on October 8–9, and the October 9 show proved why this project remains essential listening for anyone who understands what Japanese rock can achieve when veterans refuse to play it safe.

For those unfamiliar: hide, the late guitarist and frontman of X Japan, left an indelible mark on visual rock before his death in 1998. hide with Spread Beaver—a rotating ensemble featuring his longtime collaborators—exists to honor his legacy while pushing it forward. This tour pairs hide's vocals (courtesy of archive recordings and performance techniques developed specifically for this project) with live musicianship that captures his restless spirit.

![hide with Spread Beaver performing at Zepp Haneda](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F10123322%2FALL_TK63097-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The October 9 setlist was a masterclass in pacing. After Kiyoshi Seiji (ZEPPET STORE) warmed the crowd with stripped-down hide covers, including an unreleased track called "TECHNO POP" with newly-written lyrics, the full band crashed through "Pink Spider" like a lightning strike. The heavyweight assault continued with "BACTERIA" and "DOUBT"—songs that don't ask permission, they demand surrender.

What elevated the night was the guest appearance by PATA, hide's X Japan bandmate, whose blues-tinged guitar work transformed "CELEBRATION" into an unbridled celebration of pure rock and roll freedom. The contrast worked brilliantly: moments of theatrical, emotional depth (the piano-driven "FLAME" performed by Kiyoshi with genuine vulnerability) colliding against sonic sledgehammers.

![Kiyoshi performing "FLAME" at Zepp Haneda](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F10123322%2FKIMURA_K5_2146-683x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The setlist cycled through rockers like "MISERY" and closer "POSE," each one hitting harder than the last. This isn't nostalgia wrapped in reverb—this is active, living rock music that proves hide's vision transcends the era he helped define.

For tickets, lineup details, and upcoming dates on the "REPSYCLE THE REPSYCLED" tour, check hide with Spread Beaver's official channels and authorized ticket vendors. This is essential viewing for anyone who believes rock music still has teeth.
