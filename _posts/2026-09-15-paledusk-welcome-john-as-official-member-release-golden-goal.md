Paledusk have made major announcements during their appearance on All Night Nippon MUSIC WEEK, unveiling both a bold new single and a significant lineup evolution.

The band revealed that John, who has been supporting them as a touring musician, is now officially joining Paledusk as a full member. Fresh artist photographs showcasing the band's new five-piece configuration have been released alongside the announcement. This marks an important moment in the group's trajectory as they move into their next creative chapter with a solidified roster.

![Paledusk new lineup announcement](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F14234133%2F1-18.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Accompanying this news is the announcement of a fresh single, "GOLDEN GOAL," arriving September 23rd across all major streaming platforms. The track draws inspiration from the band's real-world experience witnessing soccer matches in the UK — that visceral stadium energy translates directly into the song's DNA. Paledusk crafted something that balances weighty instrumentation with an unexpectedly exhilarating chorus, channeling the intensity of packed arenas into pure rock momentum.

Thematically, "GOLDEN GOAL" tells a fantastical story of relentless ambition: characters who pursue their dreams with hunger, constantly challenging themselves to break through barriers. The "golden goal" itself serves as a metaphor for finding your true place in the world — that elusive destination where you finally belong.

![Single artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F14234140%2F2-6-1024x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

This release kicks off an ambitious tour schedule spanning early 2027, with Paledusk hitting major cities from Fukuoka to Tokyo. Venues range from intimate live houses to arena stages like Zepp Nagoya and Zepp Osaka, signaling serious momentum heading into next year.

Head to the official streaming link to hear "GOLDEN GOAL" on September 23rd, and keep an eye on Paledusk's official channels for tour ticket information and further updates.
