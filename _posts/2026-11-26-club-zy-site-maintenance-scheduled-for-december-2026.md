If you've been hunting for Visual Kei live tickets through Club Zy's platform, mark your calendars—the ticketing service is going offline for essential system upgrades next month.

Club Zy, one of Japan's major live event ticketing and information hubs, has announced scheduled maintenance that will temporarily take the site offline. The work is set to run on **Thursday, December 10th, 2026, from 1:00 AM to 6:00 AM Japan Standard Time**. The venue notes that the end time may shift depending on how the work progresses.

For Western fans accustomed to purchasing tickets through Japanese platforms, this is a routine but important heads-up. While the maintenance window falls during early morning hours in Japan, it's worth remembering if you're planning ticket purchases around that date. The service will be completely inaccessible during the maintenance period, so it's best to handle any urgent bookings before or after that window.

Club Zy serves as a crucial infrastructure point for the Japanese live music scene, handling ticket sales, event information, and artist announcements across multiple genres—Visual Kei included. These kinds of system upgrades are essential to keep the platform running smoothly as traffic and user bases continue to grow. The team emphasizes their commitment to providing better service going forward.

If you're eyeing tickets for upcoming Visual Kei shows, performances, or related events listed on the platform, consider purchasing them in advance of the maintenance window to avoid any last-minute complications. Keep an eye on your favorite bands' official channels and social media for any tour announcements or ticket release dates, especially those that might be promoted through Club Zy's system.

Plan ahead, secure your tickets early if possible, and stay tuned to your favorite artists' official websites and social channels for the latest touring updates.
