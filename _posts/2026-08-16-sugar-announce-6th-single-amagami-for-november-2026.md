The melodic visual kei outfit sugar is gearing up to deliver fresh material this fall. The band has confirmed that their sixth single, *Amagami*, will hit shelves on November 4, 2026.

For those less familiar with sugar, the band has carved out a distinctive niche within the visual kei landscape with their blend of driving rock instrumentation and emotive vocal delivery. Over the course of their discography, they've built a loyal fanbase through consistent releases and their ability to balance accessibility with artistic substance—hallmarks that define their creative trajectory since their formation.

The announcement of *Amagami*—which translates to "sweet bite"—marks a significant milestone for the group. Reaching a sixth single represents meaningful longevity in a scene where bands often experience flux in lineup and direction. Each release has allowed them to refine their craft, and anticipation is already building around what sonic territory this new offering will explore.

While full details about the single's structure, format, and track listing have yet to be revealed, the November release window positions *Amagami* perfectly for the winter season—a time when many fans are seeking new material to absorb during the colder months ahead.

For those who've followed sugar's journey thus far, this announcement is reason to mark calendars. For newcomers curious about what the band brings to the table, *Amagami* could serve as an ideal entry point into their expanding catalog.

Keep an eye on sugar's official channels for additional information as we approach the release date. Track preorders, behind-the-scenes content, and potential promotional performances will likely emerge in the coming weeks. This is shaping up to be an exciting chapter for the band.
