DEZERT are building momentum toward a major milestone. The Tokyo-based Visual Kei act has just unveiled fresh artist photography and revealed full details for their 15th anniversary tour finale—all while preparing to drop their major second album next week.

The self-titled album "DEZERT" arrives October 28th, marking a significant moment for the band led by vocalist Senshi alongside guitarists Miyako, bassist Sacchan, and drummer SORA. The release comes in two editions: a lavish limited "Kyouseiuban" (strongest edition) package with 3 CDs and a Blu-ray, plus a standard edition containing 2 CDs and an M-Card. The strongest pressing includes over 148 minutes of live footage from their 47-area tour finale at Makuhari Messe in March, plus an exclusive 60-minute documentary about the band's creative journey.

![DEZERT band promotional image](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fdf-580x820.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The album spans 20 tracks across two discs of new material, ranging from the haunting "Welcome To The End" to introspective deep cuts like "鬱鬱鬱" (depression/melancholy). A third disc features stripped-down acoustic versions and entertaining radio sessions that showcase the band's personality beyond their heavier compositions.

Speaking of live experiences, DEZERT's nationwide tour "生きてゆこうね" (Let's Keep Living) X'MAS EDITION launches in November and culminates December 25th at EX THEATER ROPPONGI in Tokyo. The tour hits major cities including Nagoya, Osaka, Fukuoka, and Sapporo through December. Standing room and reserved seating options range from ¥7,000–¥8,000 depending on venue.

Fan club "Himawari-kai" members get first access to tickets starting today (October 13th) through October 20th, with results announced October 21–22. General ticket sales follow through the official ticketing system.

To get a taste of what's coming, DEZERT recently released live footage of "Welcome To The End" from their recent Kanadevia Hall performance in Tokyo. It's a perfect introduction to the new album's aesthetic.

Head to DEZERT's official website for complete tour information, ticket links, and pre-order details. The album is available for pre-add and pre-save across all major streaming platforms. Follow their Instagram, X/Twitter, and YouTube channel for ongoing 15th anniversary project updates.
