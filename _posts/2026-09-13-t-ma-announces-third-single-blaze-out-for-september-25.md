A rising star is making serious waves before officially stepping into the spotlight. TØ-MA, a 2.5-dimensional singer who straddles the line between reality and virtual performance, will release their third single "BLAZE OUT" on September 25, 2026.

Launching their career in February 2026, TØ-MA has already caught the attention of major Visual Kei figures. Their debut track "Gerbera" featured production from Skyscraper Opera guitarist Yusuke and involved recording work from both IKUO and Ayasa (East of Eden). The MV brought in Kagemusya from -Shintenjigakushudan- Zigzag to complete an all-star debut effort. With a vocal range spanning three octaves or more, TØ-MA's signature sound combines nostalgic warmth with a charming, sensual vocal presence.

"BLAZE OUT" elevates this momentum considerably. The composition and arrangement come courtesy of GAK, a rising creative force who works as a guitarist for Takaaki Nishikawa and as part of the composer unit amazuti. TØ-MA wrote the lyrics themselves. The instrumental backbone features Rayflower's YUKI on guitar and La'crya Christi's SHUSE on bass—two seasoned musicians with deep Acid Black Cherry connections. Additional drum arrangement support comes from Skyscraper Opera's Dr. Hibiki.

Rather than retreading the debut's territory, "BLAZE OUT" swings toward explosive rock energy without sacrificing TØ-MA's melodic strengths. Aggressive and propulsive, yet distinctly charming—a promising balance for what comes next.

TØ-MA's first headline appearance comes September 29 at "Bugusami 2026 Tokyo" DAY1, a BugLug-curated festival held at Club Asia in Tokyo. The slot runs 12:00–12:30. Pre-sale tickets are ¥9,000; day-of entry is ¥10,000 (both plus processing fees). All venues offer free entry/exit with capacity limits.

Grab tickets through the official link and mark your calendar for the single drop. This project deserves your attention.
