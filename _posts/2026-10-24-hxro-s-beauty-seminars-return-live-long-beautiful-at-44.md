If you've been in the Visual Kei scene long enough, you've probably heard Hxro's name whispered among the beauty-conscious contingent of the fandom. The ex-ν[NEU] guitarist turned beauty entrepreneur has become something of a cult figure—and for good reason. Now, as 2026 winds down, Hxro is bringing back the year's final installment of his beloved "Beautiful Aging While Living for Live Music Until 100" beauty seminars, and honestly, the premise alone is refreshingly honest in a scene obsessed with eternal youth.

Hxro, who'll be celebrating his 44th birthday on December 3rd, has spent the past year holding regular seminars that blend practical skincare advice with a philosophy that resonates deeply with dedicated concertgoers: take care of yourself *now* so you can keep living for the music later. It's less about chasing an impossible standard of beauty and more about sustainable self-care as a form of devotion to your lifestyle.

The seminars are hitting two cities this December. Tokyo's Culture Culture will host the first event on December 6th (doors 11:30, start 12:00), with Osaka's Loft PlusOne West following on December 26th (doors 12:30, start 13:00). Both events wrap by 15:00—a thoughtful touch that lets attendees hit an evening live afterward if they wish.

The ticket price is almost shockingly reasonable: just 500 yen, and that includes a full-size Bloom Clay Cleansing product from DearLily, Hxro's own skincare line. There's also a special birthday twist: Hxro is giving away his gift *to* attendees rather than the other way around. You'll even get a chance to meet him for a photo and hand-deliver birthday messages or gifts if you wish.

Pre-sale registration closes October 25th at 23:59, with general sales opening November 8th at 12:00.

For tickets and full event details, head to the official links on Tiget. Follow Hxro on his official X account for any last-minute updates. If you've ever wondered how to keep showing up for live music without burning out—or simply want skincare advice from someone who actually lives it—this is worth your time and yen.
