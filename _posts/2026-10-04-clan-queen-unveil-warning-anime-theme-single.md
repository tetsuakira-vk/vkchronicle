CLAN QUEEN have kicked off the countdown to their highly anticipated single release with early streaming access to "WARNING!!," their new opening theme for the TV anime *PSYREN*. The track is everything you'd expect from a band operating at maximalist overdrive: alarm sounds pierce through aggressive guitar riffs that establish momentum from the first second, before the song unfolds into a propulsive alternative soundscape. The chorus channels pure kinetic energy—imagery of unleashed imagination and creativity crystallized into something that feels both urgent and exhilarating.

![CLAN QUEEN WARNING!! single artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F28184723%2F260910_CQ_WARNING_%25E9%2585%258D%25E4%25BF%25A1JKT_FIX.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The anime's non-credited opening sequence will premiere on October 5th at 7:00 AM JST via the official *It's Anime* YouTube channel, giving fans their first visual pairing with the track ahead of the broadcast proper.

"WARNING!!" arrives in two distinct formats when it drops on November 11th. The complete production limited edition pairs the CD with a Blu-ray containing the full concert footage from CLAN QUEEN's sold-out May 15th, 2026 show at Zepp DiverCity Tokyo as part of their "Grand Hotel MONOPOLY Tour"—marking the first time the band has packaged live visual material alongside a release. The period-limited CD-only edition comes housed in a 12-inch LP-sized package featuring original artwork from manga creator Toshiaki Iwashiro.

![CLAN QUEEN complete production limited edition](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F28184723%2F260925_CQ_WARNING_%25E5%25AE%258C%25E5%2585%25A8%25E7%2594%259F%25E7%2594%25A3%25E7%259B%25A4.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Beyond this single, CLAN QUEEN are gearing up for their inaugural hall tour this spring, spanning five cities from April 11th through May 29th, 2027. Presale tickets through their "Hell Fire Club" fanclub are already live. The band continues to build momentum with each release, and "WARNING!!" feels like another essential chapter in their catalog.

Stream the advance release now and stay locked into CLAN QUEEN's official channels for all upcoming details on merchandise, tour ticketing, and additional content.
