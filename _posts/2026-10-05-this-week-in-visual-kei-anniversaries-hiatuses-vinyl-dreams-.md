What a week it's been—equal parts celebration and bittersweet goodbyes. The Visual Kei scene kicked off October with the kind of momentum that reminds us why we're all obsessed: milestone anniversaries, surprise anime collaborations, and enough new music to keep your playlists spinning through next month.

Let's start with the wins. Angelo marked 20 years of existence with a triumphant "Angel's Day" finale that had fans emotional and reinvigorated. It's the kind of anniversary show that feels less like a victory lap and more like a promise of what's still to come. Meanwhile, L'Arc-en-Ciel proved that legacy never gets old, announcing vinyl reissues of 12 classic albums—a love letter to longtime fans and a gateway drug for newcomers who want their Visual Kei on wax.

New releases dominated the headlines this week too. ELISE dropped a self-titled mini-album that showcases their masterful grasp of Visual Kei aesthetics, while XaaXaa's "KESYOU" music video dug deep into identity and transformation—exactly the kind of conceptual depth we crave. CLAN QUEEN surprised everyone with an anime theme single called "WARNING!!"—proof that Visual Kei's reach into mainstream media keeps expanding. And DIV hit us with "Vampire's Applause," a moody single that feels bittersweet knowing it arrives ahead of their announced recharge hiatus.

On the live front, things were equally electric. Sadie and DEZERT absolutely ignited Yokohama with a fiery co-headline that had everyone talking, while The THIRTEEN kept the energy festive with a lineup of member collabs and events—including some fashion-forward moments that show why Mao and Mizuki remain scene staples.

There's something uniquely Visual Kei about this week: the nostalgia of vinyl and anniversaries sitting comfortably alongside the innovation of anime tie-ins and transformative new singles. It's a scene that honors its roots while refusing to stay stuck in the past.

Next week, keep your eyes peeled for more tour announcements and the fallout from DIV's hiatus news—this could reshape some autumn schedules.
