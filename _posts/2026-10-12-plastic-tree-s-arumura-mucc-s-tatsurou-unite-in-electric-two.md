When two solo artists from legendary Japanese rock bands share a stage, magic happens—and the October 7th performance at Tokyo Kinema Club proved exactly why fans have been anticipating this collaboration for nearly two years.

Arumura Ryuutarou (frontman of Plastic Tree) and Tatsurou (MUCC's bassist and vocalist) reunited for the second installment of their two-man live event, *Arumura Ryuu Tatsurou*, drawing devoted audiences across two Tokyo dates (October 6–7) before heading to Nagoya's JAMMIN' venue on October 13–14. The expanded tour marks a significant step up from their inaugural run.

Tatsurou opened the evening with a masterclass in controlled intensity. From the hard-hitting intro of "Kimi na Kajitsu" through set-closer "Rakka no Shuuchakuten," he commanded the room with equal parts elegance and raw power. His vocal range spanned from intimate whispers to piercing shouts, while the band's crystalline guitar work and relentless rhythm section kept the floor unified. A Plastic Tree cover of "Reset" added collaborative flavor, with the crowd erupting at this cross-project nod.

![Tatsurou performs at the two-man event](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F12151309%2F3.%25E9%2580%25B9%25E7%2591%25AF_2026_261011_3-683x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Arumura's solo segment showcased the ethereal, introspective side of his artistry. Opening with "≒engeki," he immediately shifted the venue's atmosphere—floating vocal melodies over shifting time signatures created an almost dreamlike state. Highlights included the shimmeringly beautiful "Koi to Maboroshi" and an audacious MUCC cover of "Mama," each performance pulling deeper emotional responses from an already-engaged audience.

A special moment emerged when bassist Seek (Psycho le Cému) stepped in as an emergency substitute for the originally scheduled bassist, missing the release day of his own first solo album to honor the commitment. Arumura's on-stage shout-out—"The man, Seek!"—drew roaring approval.

![The full band lineup performs together](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2F12151308%2F1.%25E6%259C%2589%25E6%259D%2591%25E7%25AB%259C%25E9%2580%25B9%25E7%2591%25AF2026_261011_1-1024x683.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The night's most memorable moment came mid-set when Tatsurou paused to address the crowd: "This two-man show—it feels like something beyond just two artists. The stage and floor have become one. Thank you for that." His words captured the essence of what makes these collaborations irreplaceable.

The encore featured a *cali≠gari* cover ("Siren") before both artists took a final bow to thunderous applause and waving towels.
