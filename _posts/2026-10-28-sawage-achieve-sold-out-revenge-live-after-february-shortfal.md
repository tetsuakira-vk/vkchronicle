When a band falls short of their own expectations, the real test isn't accepting defeat—it's having the conviction to try again. That's exactly what dance and vocal group SAWAGE did this September, and the results told a story worth hearing.

Back in February, SAWAGE held their 6th Anniversary One Man Live at Kashiwa PALOOЗА with an ambitious 300-person capacity target. Around 260 fans showed up, which might sound respectable to most bands. But SAWAGE's members—Katsuumi Sato, Kiki, and Masahisa—weren't satisfied. They publicly declared that true success meant a sold-out show, not just a decent turnout. That conviction led directly to their September 30th "Revenge One Man Live," and this time, they delivered.

![SAWAGE performing with live band at revenge show](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fvijuttoke.com%2Fcontents%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fsaw-580x387.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

The September show reinforced why this trio deserves your attention. Working alongside an elite backing band—SHOJI on guitar (ex-FAME, ex-FIX), Yanagi on bass (ex-少年アジト), and HIMAWARI on drums (ex-SEX MACHINEGUNS, DUSTAR-3)—SAWAGE crafted a performance that swung from explosive rock energy to genuine emotional depth. Their setlist pulled from hide's "ROCKET DIVE" (a connection they earned after performing at a hide tribute event earlier this year) and their own catalog, which spans everything from floor-shaking bangers like "ガチアガレ" and "KING" to surprisingly tender moments like "ダイヤモンド" and "ラブソングが�意えない・・・"

What struck most observers was SAWAGE's range. These aren't one-trick performers. They can ignite a room with dance-rock fury, then pull the crowd into genuine connection through careful harmonies and balladic storytelling. Their TikTok presence—particularly "君はハンパないッ！" with 170,000 views—shows they're building momentum beyond the live circuit.

Equally telling was the rapport between SAWAGE and their backing musicians. During the MC segment, the three revealed they now socialize with their band—even taking BBQ trips together. That kind of chemistry translates instantly to an audience.

If you haven't caught SAWAGE yet, their streaming numbers (currently at 150,000 plays) suggest plenty of Western fans are still discovering them. Check out their official channels and streaming platforms to explore their full catalog—this is a band that rewards deep listening.
