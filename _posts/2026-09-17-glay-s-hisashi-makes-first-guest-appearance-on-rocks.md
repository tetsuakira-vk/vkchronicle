One of Japan's most enduring rock institutions is heading to the streaming spotlight next week. GLAY's legendary guitarist HISASHI will make his debut appearance on the popular music information live stream いじくりROCKS on September 24, 2026, and the anticipation is already building.

For those new to the band: GLAY has been a pillar of the Japanese rock scene since their major debut in 1994. Over three decades, they've sold millions of records and maintained a devoted global fanbase—a rare feat for any band, let alone one that refuses to compromise their artistic vision. HISASHI's guitar work has been central to their sound, blending technical proficiency with unmistakable melodic sensibility.

The broadcast will be hosted by Tatsurou (MUCC) and Dancho (NoGoD), with YUKKE from MUCC joining as a panelist. This lineup is particularly significant: YUKKE has long cited GLAY as a major influence, so expect the kind of in-depth, reverent conversation that only comes from genuine artistic kinship. The show promises more than surface-level banter—we're talking the kind of deep-cut discussion that GLAY fans crave.

Timing-wise, this is a peak moment for GLAY. They're releasing their 64th single, "Kegare ta Eiyuu" (Tainted Hero), the day before the broadcast, and they've got the "GLAY ARENA TOUR 2026-2027 'EXOFIRE'" lined up starting in November. Translation: HISASHI will have plenty to discuss about where the band is headed creatively.

The stream kicks off at 7 PM JST on September 24 via YouTube and Niconico Live, with exclusive member-only content during the second half of the broadcast. If you want to catch a living legend discuss his craft and the current state of Japanese rock, this is unmissable.

Tune in to hear what HISASHI has to say about the past, present, and future of one of Japan's most important rock bands. Check GLAY's official channels for tour details and the latest news on their upcoming releases.
