Hump Back is gearing up for a major chapter as they unveil their third mini album *Soft Cave* (やわらかな洞窟), arriving October 14, 2026—their first release under the Echoes label.

The eight-track project features artwork by illustrator KICHI, who has shaped Hump Back's live visuals and creative direction for years. The cover centers on a cave motif that mirrors the album's poetic title, creating an atmospheric gateway into the band's latest sonic terrain.

![Hump Back album artwork](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F09%2F05114705%2FHumpBack_YawarakanaDokutsu_mini.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

For those unfamiliar, Hump Back has carved out a distinctive presence in the contemporary Japanese rock landscape with emotionally textured songwriting and dynamic live energy. This mini album pulls together recent highlights alongside fresh material—"To That Town" began as a JR Central commercial theme before receiving a lyrical rewrite for this release. The tracklist also includes "Orange Light," the band's latest single, plus "Love and Rock n' Roll," written specifically for TV Tokyo's *Shina Pushu* program. "Gurled" makes its official debut after a limited run at their Osaka Castle Hall show in May.

The full eight-track roster reads: "To That Town," "Gurled," "Driftwood Finale," "Mummy Brain," "Merry-Go-Round," "I've Never Done Anything Seriously in My Life," "Love and Rock n' Roll," and "Orange Light."

![Tour poster](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnew-img.barks.jp%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F05%2F16172137%2FHumpBack_2026-27Tour-819x1024.jpg&w=800&output=jpg)

Coinciding with the album news, Hump Back has announced the *Hello Anew Tour 2026-2027*, kicking off November 1 at Zepp Osaka Bayside. The band will hit major cities including Nagoya, Sapporo, Fukuoka, Sendai, and Tokyo through November, with an international stop in Seoul on January 23, 2027. General ticket sales opened September 5, with prices ranging from ¥4,000–¥6,000 depending on seating and membership status.

The standard edition CD arrives in a premium slipcase package at ¥3,000 (tax included). Early purchasers through major retailers will receive exclusive bonuses—details dropping soon.

For pre-orders, ticket details, and tour information, head to the official Hump Back channels and ticketing partners. This is shaping up to be a defining moment for the band.
